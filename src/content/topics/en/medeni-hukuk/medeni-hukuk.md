## Core idea

Civil law is the core branch of private law governing people's private lives from birth to death. The Turkish Civil Code (*TMK*, Law 4721) has introductory provisions and books on persons, family, succession and property (the law of obligations is in a separate code). The test has 6 questions; they usually ask about capacity, the rules of marriage and divorce, the order of heirs and real rights.

## Introductory provisions

- **Application of law:** the code applies to every matter its wording and spirit cover; if there is no provision, **custom** applies, and failing that the judge makes a rule **as if a legislator**. Scholarly opinion and case law serve as guidance.
- **Rule of honesty** (*dürüstlük kuralı*): everyone must act honestly when exercising rights and performing duties; the law does not protect the **manifest abuse** of a right.
- **Good faith** (*iyiniyet*): where a right depends on good faith, good faith is **presumed**.
- **Burden of proof:** as a rule, whoever asserts a fact must prove it.

## Law of persons

- **Legal capacity** (*hak ehliyeti*): begins with complete and live birth and ends with death; a child has legal capacity from conception, provided it is born alive.
- **Capacity to act** (*fiil ehliyeti*): requires **power of discernment** (*ayırt etme gücü*), **majority** and **not being under interdiction**. Majority begins at eighteen; marriage and being declared of age by a court (*kazai rüşt*) also confer majority.
- **Capacity groups:** fully capable, of limited capacity, of **limited incapacity** (*sınırlı ehliyetsiz*: minors and interdicted persons who have discernment) and fully incapable (those without discernment).
- **Legal persons:** associations of persons (**association**, *dernek*) and dedicated assets (**foundation**, *vakıf*).

## Family and succession law

- **Defective marriage:** **absolute nullity** (*mutlak butlan*, for example marrying while already married or between close relatives) is a grave defect the prosecutor raises of their own motion and any interested person may sue on; with **relative nullity** (*nispi butlan*, defects of will such as mistake, deceit or duress) only the spouse whose will was impaired may sue.
- **Divorce:** special grounds (adultery, attempt on life and degrading treatment, crime and dishonourable life, desertion, mental illness) and the general ground of the **fundamental breakdown of the marriage**.
- **Statutory property regime:** **participation in acquired property** (*edinilmiş mallara katılma*).
- **Protective institutions:** parental custody (*velayet*), guardianship (*vesayet*), curatorship (*kayyımlık*) and legal advisorship.
- **Statutory heirs:** the **parentelic system** (*zümre*): first descendants, then parents and their descendants, then grandparents; the surviving spouse inherits alongside each group; without heirs the estate goes to the state.
- **Reserved share** (*saklı pay*): granted to descendants, parents and the spouse; the deceased cannot remove these shares by their dispositions.
- **Dispositions upon death:** the will (*vasiyetname*: official, handwritten, oral) and the inheritance contract.

## Property law

- **Possession** (*zilyetlik*): actual control over a thing; distinct from ownership.
- **Acquiring ownership:** for movables, as a rule **delivery**; for immovables, **registration in the land registry** (*tapu sicili*).
- **Limited real rights:** servitudes, land charges and pledges/mortgages.

## Commonly confused

- *Legal capacity* is being able to hold rights and every human has it; *capacity to act* is being able to acquire rights and take on debts through one's own acts.
- The *rule of honesty* is an objective standard of conduct; *good faith* is a person's not knowing of a defect.
- *Possession* is actual control; *ownership* is legal control.

## Study tip

In capacity questions, check discernment first, then majority, then interdiction; this order gives you the person's group at once. Always check age limits and inheritance shares in the current TMK on mevzuat.gov.tr.
