## Core idea

Physical education in special education (*özel eğitimde fiziksel eğitim ve spor*) aims to develop students' movement skills, health and social participation. The approach is called **adapted physical education**: the student is not made to fit the activity; **the activity is adapted to the student**. There are **2 questions**; they ask about types of adaptation and safety precautions.

## Movement skills

- **Locomotor skills:** walking, running, jumping, sliding.
- **Object control skills:** throwing, catching, kicking, dribbling.
- **Stability skills:** turning, bending, balancing on one foot.
- **Physical fitness:** muscular strength, endurance, flexibility, cardiovascular endurance.

## Ways of adapting

- **Equipment:** a bigger, lighter or sound-emitting ball; a lowered basket.
- **Space:** a smaller court, clear boundaries.
- **Rules:** allowing a double bounce, changing the number of players.
- **Time and intensity:** shorter activities, frequent rests.
- **Teaching:** visual models, physical prompts, peer support; skills are broken into steps by task analysis.

## Safety and types of disability

- **Down syndrome:** because of the risk of **atlantoaxial instability**, activities that load the neck (such as forward rolls) require medical clearance.
- **Visual impairment:** sound balls, tactile boundaries, guide runners; **goalball** is a team sport developed for athletes with visual impairment.
- **Epilepsy, asthma, heart conditions:** intensity and duration follow medical advice.

## Organisations

- **Special Olympics:** for athletes with intellectual disability.
- **Paralympic Games:** elite competition for athletes with physical and visual impairments (and, in some events, intellectual disability).
- Separate **Deaflympics** are held for athletes with hearing impairment.

## Commonly confused

- *Adapted physical education* is teaching at school; *disability sport* is competition-oriented.
- A *rule adaptation* changes how the game works; an *equipment adaptation* changes the materials used.

## Study tip

For an adaptation example ask "what changed: equipment, space, rules or time?"; the question usually wants that classification.
