## Core idea

In social studies teaching, geography teaches the **two-way interaction between people and the natural environment** through general concepts and especially the example of **Turkey**. To explain where something is found, think in order of location, landforms and climate. The test has 12 questions; they usually ask about reading maps and graphs, Turkey's physical and human features, and disasters.

## General geography

- **Maps:** **scale**, contour and coloured relief maps; a large-scale map shows more detail and a smaller area.
- **Location:** **absolute (mathematical) location** is given by latitude and longitude; **relative location** concerns neighbours, seas and transport routes.
- **Motions of the Earth:** daily rotation causes day and night and differences in **local time**; yearly revolution and axial tilt cause the seasons.
- **Climate elements:** temperature, pressure, wind, humidity and precipitation, shaped by latitude, altitude, distance from the sea and aspect.

## Turkey's physical geography

- **Absolute location:** roughly between 36°–42° north and 26°–45° east; there is a **76-minute** local time difference between its eastern and western ends.
- **Landforms:** a young, high country on the **Alpine-Himalayan** belt; the North Anatolian Mountains in the north, the **Taurus** in the south, horst-and-graben structure in western Anatolia; the **North Anatolian** and **East Anatolian** fault lines raise earthquake risk.
- **Climate:** **Mediterranean** and **Black Sea** climates on the coasts, **continental** climate inland; mountains running parallel to the coasts make the interior drier.
- **Vegetation:** **maquis** on the Mediterranean coast, humid **forests** on the Black Sea, **steppe** in Central Anatolia.
- **Waters:** rivers are steep with irregular regimes; **Lake Van** is the largest lake and is alkaline (soda); there are **closed basins** in Central Anatolia and the Lakes Region.

## Human geography, disasters and environment

- **Population and migration:** people concentrate on the coasts, plains and industrial centres; **rural-urban migration** sped up urbanisation.
- **Economy:** climatic variety gives crop variety; industry concentrates around the Marmara; tourism has grown on the coasts.
- **Disasters:** earthquakes (fault lines), **landslides** (slope and rain on the Black Sea coast), floods, avalanches and forest fires (Mediterranean coast).
- **Environment:** air and water pollution, erosion, desertification; **sustainability** and disaster awareness are key aims of social studies teaching.

## Commonly confused

- *Absolute location* is given by latitude and longitude; *relative location* by relations with surroundings.
- *Maquis* is the vegetation of the Mediterranean climate; *steppe* that of the continental climate.
- A *landslide* is mass movement on a slope; *erosion* is soil carried away by water and wind.

## Study tip

Draw Turkey's mountain ranges, fault lines and climate regions on top of each other on one map. Most population, farming and disaster questions are solved by reading these three layers together.
