## Core idea

*Tefsir* (tafsir) is the discipline that explains the meaning of Qur'anic verses as far as human ability allows; *te'vil* (ta'wil) is choosing, with evidence, one of the possible meanings of a word. Doing tafsir requires the Qur'anic sciences (occasions of revelation, abrogation, the Meccan-Medinan distinction). The test has 5 questions; they usually ask you to match works, exegetes and methods, or to define concepts from *Ulumu'l-Kur'an* (the Qur'anic sciences).

## Qur'anic sciences

- ***Esbab-ı nüzul*** (occasions of revelation): the event or question that prompted a verse; it helps understand context.
- **Meccan and Medinan:** Meccan verses mostly treat monotheism, the hereafter and ethics; Medinan verses treat law, social order and relations with the People of the Book.
- ***Nasih and mensuh*** (abrogating and abrogated): one ruling lifted by a later one; its scope is debated among scholars.
- ***Muhkem and müteşabih***: verses with a clear meaning and verses open to several meanings (Al Imran 3:7).

## Methods and history

- **Early period:** the first explainer was the Prophet; among the Companions **Ibn Abbas**, **Ibn Mas'ud** and **Ubayy ibn Ka'b** stand out. In the Successors' generation, centres formed in Mecca, Medina and Kufa.
- ***Rivayet*** (transmission-based) **tafsir:** explains verses by other verses, hadith and the words of Companions and Successors. Examples: **Tabari**, *Jami' al-Bayan*; **Ibn Kathir**, *Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Azim*.
- ***Dirayet*** (reason-based) **tafsir:** foregrounds language, reasoning and the sciences. Examples: **Zamakhshari**, *al-Kashshaf* (language and rhetoric; Mu'tazili); **Fakhr al-Din al-Razi**, *Mafatih al-Ghayb*; **Maturidi**, *Ta'wilat al-Qur'an*.
- **Legal tafsir** focuses on verses of rulings (e.g. **Qurtubi**); ***işari*** (allusive) tafsir is the Sufi practice of pointing to inner meanings alongside the outward one.
- **Contemporary approaches:** **thematic tafsir**, tafsir in order of revelation, social tafsir (*al-Manar*). In Turkish: **Elmalılı Hamdi Yazır**, *Hak Dini Kur'an Dili*.

## Commonly confused

- *Tafsir* explains meaning; *ta'wil* chooses one possible meaning with evidence.
- *Transmission-based* tafsir relies on reports, *reason-based* tafsir on language and reasoning; the latter does not reject reports.
- *Muhkam* verses have a clear meaning, *mutashabih* verses are open to several.

## Study tip

Put the major exegetes in a three-column table of work, method and period; most questions come from this matching.
