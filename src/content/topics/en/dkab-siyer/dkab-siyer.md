## Core idea

*Siyer* (sirah) is the discipline that tells the life of the Prophet; *megazi* (maghazi) tells the campaigns he took part in. The first systematic sirah is the work of **Ibn Ishaq**, which reached us in **Ibn Hisham**'s edition. The test has 3 questions; they usually ask about the order of events, cause and effect, and the difference between the Meccan and Medinan periods.

## The Meccan period

- ***Cahiliye*** (Jahiliyya, pre-Islamic era): tribal order, idol worship and blood feuds; as a young man Muhammad joined the ***Hılfu'l-fudul***, a pact against injustice.
- **First revelation:** came in the cave of **Hira**. In the early years the call was private; **Dar al-Arqam** became a centre of teaching and meeting.
- **Pressure:** some Muslims migrated to **Abyssinia**; a boycott was imposed on the Banu Hashim.
- **Year of Sorrow:** the deaths of Khadija and Abu Talib, followed by the journey to Ta'if and the Isra and Mi'raj.
- **Pledges of Aqaba:** two agreements with Medinan Muslims prepared the ground for the Hijra.

## The Medinan period

- **Hijra (622):** the start of the Hijri calendar. Mosques were built in Quba and Medina; ***muahat*** (brotherhood) was established between the emigrants and the helpers.
- **Constitution of Medina:** a written document setting rules of shared life among Muslims, Jews and other groups.
- **Campaigns:** **Badr** (year 2), **Uhud** (year 3), **the Trench** (year 5).
- **Treaty of Hudaybiyya** (year 6): despite seemingly harsh terms, it brought peace and eased the spread of the message.
- **Conquest of Mecca** (year 8), **Tabuk** (year 9), **Farewell Pilgrimage** (year 10); death (year 11).
- The **qibla** was changed from Jerusalem to the Ka'ba in year 2.

## Commonly confused

- *Sirah* covers the Prophet's whole life, *maghazi* only his campaigns.
- The *pledges of Aqaba* came before the Hijra, the *Constitution of Medina* after it.
- The *migration to Abyssinia* was a group's move in the Meccan period; the *Hijra* was the community's move to Medina.

## Study tip

Build a one-line timeline using Hijri years and write one result next to each event. As a teacher, also get used to drawing a value from each event (patience, justice, peace).
