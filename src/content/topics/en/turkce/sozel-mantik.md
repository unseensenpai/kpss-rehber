## Core idea

Verbal logic questions need no grammar; they need you to **turn the given conditions into an orderly table**. In the KPSS General Ability test, **questions 27–30** have come in every exam since 2018 as a group based on the same information but answered independently. Build the information correctly once and you can answer all four questions from the same table.

## Question types

- **Ordering:** people or objects are put in order (height, age, arrival, floor number). Conditions read like "A is before B" or "there is one person between C and D".
- **Placement:** people are placed in specific spots (table, room, day).
- **Matching:** each person is matched with a feature (job, colour, city).
- **Grouping:** people are split into groups by rules.

## How to solve

1. **Draw a table:** empty boxes for ordering; a grid of rows and columns for matching.
2. **Place the certain facts:** conditions like "A is third" go in first.
3. **Mark the constraints:** show facts like "B is not in the first two places" with a cross in the table.
4. **Branch the possible cases:** if several options remain, try each on a separate row and drop the ones that lead to a contradiction.
5. **Read the question:** each question may add its own information. That information applies **only to that question**.

## Certainly true, possible, certainly false

- **Certainly true:** holds in every possible case.
- **Possible:** holds in at least one possible case.
- **Certainly false:** holds in no possible case.
- Telling these three apart in the question stem matters as much as the right table.

## Commonly confused

- Carrying one question's extra information over to the next is the most common mistake; the questions are **independent**.
- "A is immediately before B" (adjacent) is not the same as "A is before B" (others may be between them).

## Study tip

Use the same table layout for every group; a consistent drawing habit lowers the chance of mistakes under exam stress. Redo solved groups against the clock: the goal is to finish all four questions in a few minutes.
