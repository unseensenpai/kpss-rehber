## Core idea

Modern physics begins where classical physics fails: at **very high speeds** (relativity) and at **very small scales** (quantum theory). The main ideas are that the speed of light is the same for every observer, energy is exchanged in packets, and matter also behaves like a wave. There are **10** questions in the test. They mostly ask what an experiment proved and include short calculations.

## Relativity and the birth of quantum theory

- **Postulates of special relativity:** the laws of physics are the same for all inertial observers; the speed of light in vacuum, c ≈ 3·10⁸ m/s, is the same for every observer.
- **Time dilation:** Δt = γ·Δt₀; **length contraction:** L = L₀/γ; γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²).
- **Mass-energy:** rest energy E₀ = m·c²; total energy E = γ·m·c².
- **Blackbody radiation:** Wien's law λ_max·T = constant; Stefan-Boltzmann P = σ·A·T⁴. Planck solved the "ultraviolet catastrophe" by assuming energy is emitted in packets E = h·f.

## Photons and matter waves

- **Photoelectric effect:** KE_max = h·f − φ. Electrons are emitted only above the threshold frequency; light intensity sets the **number** of electrons, frequency sets their **kinetic energy**.
- **Compton effect:** an X-ray scattered by an electron gets a longer wavelength: Δλ = (h/(m_e·c))·(1 − cosθ). It shows that photons carry momentum (p = h/λ).
- **de Broglie:** every particle has a wavelength λ = h/p. Electron diffraction (Davisson-Germer) confirmed it.
- **Heisenberg uncertainty principle:** Δx·Δp ≥ ħ/2; position and momentum cannot both be known exactly.

## Atom and nucleus

- **Rutherford:** the gold foil experiment showed that mass and positive charge sit in a tiny nucleus.
- **Bohr model (hydrogen):** E_n = −13.6 eV/n²; angular momentum is quantised (L = n·ħ). For the photon emitted in a transition, h·f = E_upper − E_lower.
- **Quantum mechanics:** the square of the wave function |ψ|² gives probability density; the Schrödinger equation determines ψ. Quantum numbers n, l, m_l, m_s; the Pauli principle forbids two electrons with the same four quantum numbers.
- **Nucleus:** mass defect Δm, binding energy E = Δm·c². Binding energy per nucleon peaks near iron and nickel, so light nuclei release energy by fusing and heavy nuclei by splitting.
- **Radioactive decay:** alpha (a He nucleus; A drops by 4, Z by 2), beta minus (a neutron turns into a proton; Z rises by 1), gamma (energy only). N = N₀·(1/2)^(t/T½).
- **Elementary particles:** protons and neutrons are made of quarks; the electron is a lepton. Four fundamental interactions: gravitational, electromagnetic, strong and weak.

## Commonly confused

- In the *photoelectric effect* the photon is fully absorbed; in the *Compton effect* it is scattered and loses part of its energy.
- *Fission* (*fisyon*) splits a heavy nucleus; *fusion* (*füzyon*) joins light nuclei.
- *Proper time* (Δt₀) is measured by a clock moving with the event and is the shortest; other observers measure a longer interval.

## Study tip

Reduce every experiment to one sentence answering "what did it prove?" (photoelectric: photons; Davisson-Germer: matter waves; Rutherford: the nucleus). Keeping energies in eV makes calculations easier.
