## Core idea

The **history of religions** studies religions neutrally through their origins, beliefs, practices and historical development, and takes as a principle presenting each religion as its own followers understand it. The test has 2 questions; they usually ask you to match a concept or scripture with its religion.

## Classification and Judaism

- **Classification:** by source (revealed or not), by spread (**universal**: Christianity, Islam, Buddhism; **national**: Judaism, Hinduism, Shinto), and by whether a founder is known.
- **Judaism:** scripture is the **Tanakh** (Torah, Nevi'im, Ketuvim); the **Talmud** (Mishnah and Gemara) records the oral tradition. The weekly day of rest is **Shabbat**, the place of worship the synagogue, the dietary laws *kashrut*. Festivals include Pesach, Shavuot, Sukkot and Yom Kippur. Today there are **Orthodox**, **Conservative** and **Reform** Judaism.

## Christianity

- **The Bible:** the Old and New Testaments; the four Gospels are Matthew, Mark, Luke and John.
- **Core belief:** the **Trinity** (Father, Son, Holy Spirit); the **Council of Nicaea** in 325 set Jesus' divinity as official doctrine.
- **Denominations:** in 1054 the Eastern and Western churches separated: **Catholicism** (papal authority) and **Orthodoxy**. The Reformation begun by Martin Luther in the 16th century produced **Protestantism** (priority of scripture and faith).
- **Sacraments:** sacred rites such as baptism and the Eucharist (Communion).

## Indian, East Asian and other religions

- **Hinduism:** the Vedas; **karma**, **samsara** (cycle of rebirth) and liberation (*moksha*); the caste order.
- **Buddhism:** Siddhartha Gautama (the Buddha); the Four Noble Truths, the Eightfold Path, **nirvana**.
- **Jainism** (*ahimsa*, non-violence), **Sikhism** (Guru Nanak), **Confucianism** (ethics and social order), **Taoism** (Laozi, the Tao), **Shinto** (Japan, *kami*).
- **Zoroastrianism:** the Avesta; Ahura Mazda as the god of good.
- **Ancient Turkish religion:** belief in **Gök Tanrı** (the Sky God), ancestor veneration, spirits of land and water, and *kam* (shamans).

## Commonly confused

- The *Tanakh* is the Jewish scripture, the *Talmud* the record and interpretation of oral tradition.
- *Catholicism* accepts the pope as highest authority, *Orthodoxy* does not, *Protestantism* treats scripture as the sole authority.
- *Samsara* is the cycle of rebirth, *nirvana* liberation from it.

## Study tip

For each religion, gather "founder or source, scripture, key concept, place of worship" in one table; questions usually give one of these and ask for another.
