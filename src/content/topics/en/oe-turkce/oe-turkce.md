## Core idea

The Turkish course (*Türkçe öğretimi*) develops **listening and viewing, speaking, reading and writing** together. In special education these skills are broken into steps according to the student's present level, with a focus on **functional communication**, that is, using language in daily life. There are **2 questions**; they ask about skill areas and adaptations. For early literacy see the reading and writing topic; for the components of language see the language and communication topic.

## Skill areas

- **Listening and viewing:** following instructions, finding the main idea and details of what is heard; start with short texts with visual support.
- **Speaking:** introducing oneself, expressing wishes, narrating events; supported by modelling and expansion.
- **Reading:** fluency and comprehension; texts are chosen at the student's instructional level.
- **Writing:** from words and sentences to short texts; the **writing process** (planning, drafting, revising, sharing) is taught step by step.

## Vocabulary

- New words are taught with **real objects, pictures and context**.
- **Concept maps** and meaning relations (synonyms, antonyms, categories) link words together.
- Frequent repetition and use in different contexts improve retention.

## Adaptations

- **Intellectual disability:** short, plain texts, picture support, frequent repetition.
- **Hearing impairment:** since Turkish is often acquired visually, written texts, visual support and vocabulary work come to the fore.
- **Visual impairment:** Braille, large print, audiobooks.
- **Learning disability:** strategy instruction, graphic organisers, writing frames.

## Commonly confused

- *Listening* stresses understanding a spoken message; *viewing* stresses understanding visual and auditory messages together.
- *Vocabulary teaching* focuses on word meaning; *grammar teaching* focuses on structure and rules.

## Study tip

In every activity look at what the student is doing: listening, speaking, reading or writing? The question most likely asks for that skill area or an adaptation that fits it.
