## Core idea

This subject brings together four fields built on general accounting: **cost accounting** finds what a product costs, **management accounting** uses that information for decisions, **auditing** tests whether the statements are reliable, and **TMS/TFRS** (*Türkiye Muhasebe Standartları* and *Türkiye Finansal Raporlama Standartları*, the Turkish versions of IAS and IFRS) set the rules for preparing them. Only **4 questions** come from it, so focus on the most basic concepts and formulas in each field. Questions are usually a short calculation or matching a situation with the right concept.

## Cost accounting

- **Elements of production cost:** **direct materials** (*direkt ilk madde ve malzeme*), **direct labour** (*direkt işçilik*) and **manufacturing overhead** (*genel üretim giderleri*). The first two together are **prime cost**; the last two together are **conversion cost**.
- **Job order costing:** used for distinct, customer-specific jobs (furniture, construction, printing); cost is tracked separately for each order.
- **Process costing:** used for uniform, continuous production (cement, flour); cost is collected in each production stage.
- **Equivalent units:** work in process converted into complete units according to its degree of completion; used to find unit cost in process costing.

## Management accounting

- **Variable cost** changes in total with output; **fixed cost** stays the same in total within a capacity range.
- **Contribution margin** = selling price − unit variable cost; the part left to cover fixed costs and earn profit.
- **Break-even point (units)** = total fixed costs / unit contribution margin. At this point profit is zero.
- Under **absorption costing** fixed production overhead is part of product cost; under **variable costing** it is a period expense.

## Auditing

- **Types:** **independent (external) audit**, **internal audit** and **public-sector audit**; by purpose, financial statement, compliance and operational audits.
- **Audit risk** = inherent risk × control risk × detection risk. The auditor can directly affect only **detection risk** through their own work.
- **Materiality:** a misstatement or omission large enough to change a user's decision.
- **Opinion types:** **unmodified** (*olumlu*), **qualified** (*şartlı*), **adverse** (*olumsuz*) and **disclaimer of opinion** (*görüş bildirmekten kaçınma*).

## TMS, TFRS and the conceptual framework

- In Türkiye the standards are issued by the **Public Oversight, Accounting and Auditing Standards Authority** (*KGK*).
- **Conceptual framework:** the fundamental qualitative characteristics are **relevance** and **faithful representation**.
- **Frequently asked standards:** TMS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements, TMS 2 Inventories (measured at the **lower of cost and net realisable value**), TMS 7 Statement of Cash Flows, TMS 16 Property, Plant and Equipment, TFRS 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers, TFRS 16 Leases.

## Commonly confused

- *Job order costing* is for distinct jobs; *process costing* is for uniform mass production.
- A *qualified opinion* is given for material but not pervasive problems; an *adverse opinion* for material and pervasive misstatements.
- *Prime cost* covers direct materials and direct labour; *conversion cost* covers direct labour and manufacturing overhead.

## Study tip

With so few questions, study for breadth rather than depth: put the break-even formula, when each audit opinion is given and the common standard numbers on a one-page list and review it before the exam.
