## Core idea

Constitutional law sets out the main organs of the state, how they relate to one another, and the **fundamental rights** of individuals against the state. The constitution in force is the **1982 Constitution**; the amendment approved by the 2017 referendum introduced the **presidential government system** (*Cumhurbaşkanlığı hükümet sistemi*). The test has 4 questions; they usually ask which organ holds a power, how rights may be restricted and how the Constitutional Court reviews norms.

## Basic concepts and principles

- **Rigid constitution:** one that is harder to amend than ordinary laws; the 1982 Constitution is rigid.
- **Constitutional history:** the *Kanun-i Esasi* (1876), the 1921 *Teşkilat-ı Esasiye*, and the 1924, 1961 and 1982 constitutions. The 1921 text adopts the unity of powers.
- **Unamendable articles:** the first three articles (form of the state, characteristics of the Republic, language-flag-anthem-capital) cannot be amended, and no amendment to them may even be proposed.
- **Characteristics of the Republic:** a democratic, secular and social state governed by the **rule of law** (*hukuk devleti*).

## Fundamental rights

- **Criteria for restriction:** rights may be restricted only **by law** and only for reasons the Constitution provides; a restriction may not touch the **essence** of the right and must respect **proportionality** and the requirements of a democratic society.
- **Suspension:** in war, mobilisation and states of emergency the exercise of rights may be partly or wholly suspended. Even then the right to life (except acts lawful under the law of war), physical and mental integrity, freedom of religion and conscience, the legality of crimes and penalties and the presumption of innocence remain untouchable.

## Legislature and executive

- **Grand National Assembly (*TBMM*):** 600 deputies; elections every five years, on the same day as the presidential election.
- **President:** elected directly by the people for five years and holds executive power. The President appoints vice-presidents and ministers; a deputy who is appointed loses their seat.
- **Presidential decree** (*Cumhurbaşkanlığı kararnamesi*): issued on executive matters. It cannot regulate fundamental rights, personal rights or political rights, nor matters reserved exclusively to law or already explicitly regulated by law. If a law is passed on the same matter, the decree lapses.
- **Return for reconsideration:** the President may send a law back to the Assembly; to pass it unchanged the Assembly needs an absolute majority of its total members.
- **Oversight tools:** parliamentary inquiry, general debate, parliamentary investigation and written questions.

## Judiciary and constitutional amendment

- **Constitutional Court:** 15 members; it works through **abstract review** (annulment action), **concrete review** (referral by a court) and **individual application**.
- **High courts:** the Constitutional Court, the Court of Cassation (*Yargıtay*), the Council of State (*Danıştay*) and the Court of Jurisdictional Disputes.
- **Council of Judges and Prosecutors (*HSK*):** handles the appointment, discipline and personnel matters of judges and prosecutors.
- **Amendment procedure:** a proposal needs at least one third of the Assembly's total members. An amendment passed by between three fifths and two thirds goes to a referendum unless the President sends it back; one passed by two thirds may be put to a referendum by the President.

## Commonly confused

- *Abstract review* is an annulment action brought directly by certain authorities; *concrete review* is a court referring a question that arises in a pending case.
- A *parliamentary inquiry* (*meclis araştırması*) gathers information on a subject; a *parliamentary investigation* (*meclis soruşturması*) concerns the criminal liability of the President, vice-presidents and ministers.
- *Restriction* narrows the scope of a right; *suspension* puts its exercise on hold in extraordinary periods.

## Study tip

Build a table for each organ: who elects it, who oversees it and what acts it performs; do not mix pre-2017 institutions (Council of Ministers, interpellation, decree-laws) with the current system. Always check the current text on mevzuat.gov.tr.
