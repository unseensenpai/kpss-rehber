## Core idea

Biology explains life on the basis of the **cell**: every organism is made of cells, energy is converted through photosynthesis and respiration, hereditary information is passed on by DNA, and species change through natural selection. With **13 questions** in the Science test, it surveys the field at general-science level. Questions are built around matching structure to function, comparing types of cell division, membrane transport and simple inheritance problems.

## The cell

- A **prokaryotic** cell has no membrane-bound nucleus or organelles (bacteria); a **eukaryotic** cell has a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
- **Organelles:** mitochondrion (aerobic respiration), chloroplast (photosynthesis), ribosome (protein synthesis), endoplasmic reticulum (transport and synthesis), Golgi apparatus (packaging and secretion), lysosome (intracellular digestion).
- Unlike animal cells, a **plant cell** has a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large vacuole, and usually lacks a centrosome.
- **Membrane transport:** **diffusion** goes from high to low concentration without energy; **osmosis** is the movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane; **active transport** moves substances from low to high concentration using ATP.

## Division and energy

- **Mitosis:** one division, chromosome number kept (2n → 2n), two identical cells; used for growth and repair.
- **Meiosis:** two successive divisions, chromosome number halved (2n → n), four cells; **crossing over** creates variation; produces gametes.
- **Photosynthesis:** 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (requires light and chlorophyll).
- **Aerobic respiration:** C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy (ATP). Without oxygen, fermentation occurs (lactic acid or ethanol).
- **Enzymes:** protein biological catalysts; affected by temperature and pH and specific to their substrate.

## Human body systems

- **Digestion:** chemical digestion begins in the mouth; most nutrients are absorbed in the small intestine.
- **Circulation:** the heart has four chambers; systemic circulation serves the body, pulmonary circulation the lungs.
- **Respiration:** gas exchange happens by diffusion in the alveoli.
- **Excretion:** nephrons in the kidney filter blood and form urine.
- **Control:** the nervous system gives fast control, hormones (endocrine system) slower but longer-lasting control; the goal is **homeostasis**.

## Inheritance and evolution

- **DNA** carries genetic information; a **gene** is a stretch of DNA that determines a trait.
- **Mendel:** a dominant allele masks a recessive one; an Aa × Aa cross gives a 3:1 phenotype ratio and a 1:2:1 genotype ratio.
- A **mutation** is a lasting change in DNA and a source of variation.
- **Natural selection:** individuals better suited to their environment survive and reproduce more.

## Commonly confused

- *Diffusion* is the passive movement of any substance; *osmosis* is the passive movement of water only across a membrane.
- *Mitosis* produces identical body cells; *meiosis* produces gametes with half the chromosome number.
- *Photosynthesis* stores energy in food; *respiration* transfers food energy to ATP. Plants do both.

## Study tip

Study organelles, systems and divisions as "structure → function" tables; most questions ask for this matching in different words. For inheritance, work out a few simple crosses yourself on paper.
