## Core idea

The early Turkic states were founded on the Central Asian steppe by **mounted nomads** and drew their strength from **töre** (customary law), the idea of **kut** (divine mandate) and an **army-nation** structure. Political unity usually formed around a dynasty and broke up easily when the tribes split. The test has 5 questions; they usually ask you to link a development to the right state and about concepts of government and culture.

## The Huns

- **Asian Huns:** **Mete Khan** (who came to power in 209 BC) organised the army on a **decimal system**; struggle with China is the main theme.
- **European Huns:** the **Migration Period**, which began in **375**, changed the ethnic map of Europe. Under **Attila** they were strongest against Byzantium and Western Rome.

## Göktürks and Uyghurs

- **Göktürks:** founded by **Bumin Kagan** in **552**; the first state to use "Türk" as a political name. A kagan in the east and **Istemi Yabgu** in the west: **dual organisation**.
- **Orkhon Inscriptions** (8th century): erected for Kül Tigin, Bilge Kagan and Tonyukuk; among the first major texts in the Turkic script that use the name Türk.
- **Uyghurs (744):** under **Bögü Kagan** they adopted **Manichaeism**; they settled, used paper and printing, and developed architecture and libraries. They fell to the Kyrgyz in **840**.
- **Battle of Talas (751):** between the Abbasids and China; the Karluks' support for the Abbasids sped up contact between Turks and Muslims.

## Other peoples

- The **Avars** besieged Byzantium; the ruling class of the **Khazars** adopted Judaism; one branch of the **Bulgars** founded a state on the Danube, another on the Volga; the **Pechenegs, Oghuz and Kipchaks** spread from north of the Black Sea to the Balkans.

## Culture and civilisation

- **Kut:** the belief that the right to rule is given by the Sky God (Tengri).
- **Töre:** unwritten legal rules; the ruler is bound by them too.
- **Kurultay** (*toy*): the council where state affairs were discussed; it was advisory.
- The **land** was seen as the common property of the dynasty, so succession struggles were frequent.
- **Religion and art:** belief in Tengri, shamanic practices (*kam*), **kurgan** tombs, **balbal** stones, the **animal style** and the **12-animal calendar**.

## Commonly confused

- *Kut* is the source of the right to rule; *töre* is the rules that rule must follow.
- A *kurgan* is a burial mound; a *balbal* is a stone set up at a grave.
- The *Göktürks* first made "Türk" a state name; the *Uyghurs* were the first to settle.

## Study tip

Remember one "first" for each state (decimal system, the name Türk, settled life). Questions often rely on mixing up these firsts.
