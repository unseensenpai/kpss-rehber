## Core idea

Gymnastics (*jimnastik*) is a basic sport that develops body control, strength, flexibility, balance and coordination together, and at school it lays the groundwork for many other sports. In teaching it, **safety and progression** come before anything else. The test has **2 questions**; they usually ask about branches, apparatus and teaching basic skills.

## Branches and apparatus

- **Artistic gymnastics:** 6 apparatus for men (floor, pommel horse, rings, vault, parallel bars, horizontal bar) and 4 for women (vault, uneven bars, balance beam, floor).
- **Rhythmic gymnastics:** performed to music with rope, hoop, ball, clubs and ribbon.
- **Other branches:** trampoline, aerobic, acrobatic and parkour, plus non-competitive **gymnastics for all**.

## Basic skills

- **Forward and backward roll:** chin to chest, back rounded; no weight on the head.
- **Handstand:** standing upright on the hands; taught first against a wall and with help.
- **Cartwheel** (*çember*): a sideways turn in the order hand, hand, foot, foot.
- **Bridge:** lying on the back and pushing the trunk up with hands and feet; needs shoulder and back flexibility.
- **Balances:** standing on one leg (such as the arabesque or "scale").

## Safety and teaching

- Enough **warm-up**, suitable **mats** and checked apparatus.
- **Spotting:** support from the teacher or a trained classmate so the skill can be completed safely.
- **Progression:** easy to hard, floor to apparatus, assisted to unassisted.
- Choose exercises that suit the student's level and do not frighten them; do not push a student who is not ready.

## Commonly confused

- *Artistic gymnastics* is done on apparatus; *rhythmic gymnastics* with hand apparatus and music.
- *Parallel bars* are a men's apparatus; *uneven bars* are a women's apparatus.
- *Spotting* is a safety measure; *warm-up* is preparation to prevent injury.

## Study tip

For each basic skill, write a lead-up exercise, how to spot it and a common error. In teaching-order questions, always pick the safest and easiest step first.
