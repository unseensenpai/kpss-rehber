## Core idea

This subject has two parts: the **history** of the Qur'an (revelation, writing, compilation) and *tecvid* (tajwid, the rules of correct recitation). Tajwid governs pronouncing each letter from its *mahreç* (point of articulation) with its proper **qualities**, and the rules of lengthening and merging. The test has 4 questions; they usually ask about the tajwid rule in a word or the stages of the Qur'an's compilation.

## Revelation and compilation

- **Revelation (*nüzul*):** the Qur'an came down in parts over about 23 years, in response to events and needs; verses are classed as **Meccan** and **Medinan**.
- **Collection (*cem*):** under Abu Bakr, a committee led by **Zayd ibn Thabit** gathered the written pages into a single *Mushaf* (bound copy).
- **Copying (*istinsah*):** under Uthman, this Mushaf was copied and sent to major centres to preserve a unified reading.
- **Structure:** 114 suras; suras are divided into verses and the Mushaf into 30 *cüz* (juz, parts).

## Types of madd (lengthening)

- **Madd letters:** alif, waw, ya. ***Med-i tabii*** (natural madd) is lengthened by one alif; there is no extra cause.
- ***Med-i muttasıl*** (connected madd): a hamza follows the madd letter **in the same word**; lengthening is obligatory.
- ***Med-i munfasıl*** (separated madd): the hamza is **in the next word**; lengthening is permitted.
- ***Med-i lazım*** (necessary madd): a **permanent sukun** follows the madd letter. ***Med-i arız***: the sukun arises from **pausing**.

## Sakin nun, tanwin and other rules

- ***İzhar*** (clear pronunciation): nun is pronounced clearly before the throat letters.
- ***İdgam*** (merging): with nasalisation before ya, nun, mim, waw; without it before lam and ra.
- ***İklab*** (conversion): if **ba** follows a sakin nun or tanwin, the nun turns into mim.
- ***İhfa*** (concealment): before the remaining letters, nun is concealed and nasalised.
- **Sakin mim:** *ihfa-i şefevi* before ba, *idgam-ı misleyn* before mim, *izhar-ı şefevi* elsewhere.
- ***Kalkale*** (qalqala): the echoing sound of qaf, ta, ba, jim and dal when sakin.
- **Definite lam:** pronounced before the **moon letters**, assimilated before the **sun letters**.

## Commonly confused

- *Connected madd* finds the hamza in the same word, *separated madd* in another word.
- *Iqlab* turns nun into mim, *ikhfa* conceals the nun.
- *Collection* (gathering) was under Abu Bakr, *copying* (reproduction) under Uthman.

## Study tip

Memorise each rule together with its letter group and mark a few sample words in a Mushaf. In the word given in a question, first find the sakin letter, then the letter that follows it.
