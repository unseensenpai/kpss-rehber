## Core idea

Commercial law contains rules that depart from general private law to secure the speed and reliability of business life. At the centre of the Turkish Commercial Code (*TTK*, Law 6102) is the **commercial enterprise** (*ticari işletme*): the rules on merchants, commercial transactions and the trade registry are built on it. The code also covers companies, negotiable instruments, carriage, maritime trade and insurance. The test has 6 questions; they usually ask about merchant status, the differences between company types and negotiable instruments.

## Commercial enterprise and merchant

- **Commercial enterprise:** a continuous, independent activity aiming at income above the level of a craftsman's business (*esnaf işletmesi*).
- **Natural-person merchant** (*tacir*): a person who runs a commercial enterprise, even partly, **in their own name**.
- **Legal-person merchant:** commercial companies, and foundations, associations and public bodies that run a commercial enterprise.
- **Merchant's duties:** registration in the **trade registry**, using a **trade name**, keeping commercial books and acting in all activities like a **prudent businessperson** (*basiretli iş insanı*).
- **Presumption of commercial transaction:** transactions relating to a commercial enterprise are commercial; a merchant's debts are, as a rule, presumed commercial too.
- **Trade name and business name:** the trade name (*ticaret unvanı*) is the merchant's mandatory commercial identity; the business name (*işletme adı*) identifies the enterprise.
- **Unfair competition** (*haksız rekabet*): misleading conduct, or conduct against the rule of honesty, that distorts relations between competitors or with customers.

## Companies

- **Partnerships:** in a **general partnership** (*kollektif şirket*) all partners have unlimited liability for company debts; in a **limited partnership** (*komandit şirket*) at least one partner (general partner) is unlimitedly liable and the others (limited partners) have limited liability.
- **Joint-stock company** (*anonim şirket*): capital divided into shares; shareholders are, as a rule, liable only for the capital they subscribed. Its organs are the **general assembly** and the **board of directors**; it can be formed with a single shareholder.
- **Limited liability company** (*limited şirket*): partners' liability is, as a rule, limited to the capital they subscribed; its organs are the general assembly and the managers.
- **Cooperative:** a variable-capital partnership that protects its members' economic interests through mutual help.

## Negotiable instruments

- **Negotiable instrument** (*kıymetli evrak*): a document to which a right is tied, so the right cannot be asserted without it.
- **Bills** (*kambiyo senetleri*): the **bill of exchange** (*poliçe*, a three-party order to pay: drawer, drawee and payee), the **promissory note** (*bono*, the maker's promise to pay) and the **cheque** (*çek*, drawn on a bank and payable on sight).
- **Core principles:** **strict form**, abstraction and **limitation of defences** against a holder in good faith.
- **Endorsement** (*ciro*): transferring the right by a statement written on the back and delivery.

## Commonly confused

- The *trade name* is the merchant's mandatory commercial name; the *business name* identifies the enterprise.
- In a *general partnership* all partners have unlimited liability; in a *limited partnership* liability depends on the type of partner.
- A *promissory note* is the maker's promise to pay; a *bill of exchange* is an order to pay addressed to the drawee.
