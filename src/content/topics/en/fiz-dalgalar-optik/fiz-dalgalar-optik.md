## Core idea

A wave is a disturbance that carries **energy**, not matter. Optics treats light first as rays (geometrical optics), then as a wave (physical optics). **8** questions in the test come from this subject. They focus on wave equation calculations, the Doppler effect, images from lenses and mirrors, and double-slit interference.

## Mechanical waves and sound

- **Basic relation:** v = λ·f; period T = 1/f. Wave speed depends on the medium, frequency on the source.
- **In a transverse wave** the vibration is perpendicular to propagation (string, light); **in a longitudinal wave** it is parallel (sound).
- **Speed on a stretched string:** v = √(F/μ), where μ is mass per unit length.
- **Standing wave:** on a string fixed at both ends λ = 2L/n. Adjacent nodes are λ/2 apart.
- **Pipes:** in a pipe open at both ends λ = 2L/n; a pipe closed at one end has only odd harmonics, λ = 4L/n (n odd).
- **Beats:** two close frequencies combine to give a beat frequency |f₁ − f₂|.
- **Doppler effect:** when source and observer approach, the observed frequency rises; when they separate, it falls.
- **Sound intensity level:** β = 10·log(I/I₀) decibels.

## Geometrical optics

- **Reflection:** angle of incidence equals angle of reflection.
- **Refractive index:** n = c/v. **Snell's law:** n₁·sinθ₁ = n₂·sinθ₂.
- **Total internal reflection:** happens when light goes from a denser to a less dense medium and the angle of incidence exceeds the critical angle; sinθ_c = n₂/n₁ (optical fibres).
- **Mirror and lens equation:** 1/f = 1/d_o + 1/d_i; magnification m = −d_i/d_o. For a spherical mirror f = R/2.
- **Images:** a real image is inverted and can be projected on a screen; a virtual image is upright. Plane and convex mirrors always give virtual images of real objects.

## Physical optics

- **Interference:** for two in-phase sources, a path difference of n·λ gives a bright fringe, (n + ½)·λ a dark one.
- **Young's experiment:** fringe spacing Δx = λ·L/d.
- **Single-slit diffraction:** dark fringes where a·sinθ = n·λ.
- **Thin films:** colours come from interference of rays reflected at the film's surfaces.
- **Polarisation** occurs only for transverse waves; Malus's law I = I₀·cos²θ.
- **Electromagnetic spectrum:** radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays, in order of rising frequency and shrinking wavelength.

## Commonly confused

- *Diffraction* (*kırınım*) is a wave bending around an obstacle or slit edge; *refraction* (*kırılma*) is a change of direction when it changes medium.
- A *real* image forms where rays actually meet, a *virtual* image where their extensions meet.
- When a wave passes into another medium its *frequency* stays the same; its *speed* and *wavelength* change.
