## Core idea

*Akaid* (aqidah, creed) covers the articles of faith: belief in God, angels, scriptures, prophets, the hereafter and divine decree. Its sources are the Qur'an and sound sunnah; reason is the means of understanding and defending them. The test has only 1 question; it usually asks about the definition of faith or a concept belonging to one article of faith. Detailed school debates are on the **Kalam** page.

## Faith

- **Definition:** for Ahl al-Sunnah, faith is ***tasdik*** (affirming with the heart) what the Prophet brought. For Maturidis, ***ikrar*** (declaring with the tongue) is needed for worldly legal status; some Hanafi scholars count it as a pillar of faith.
- **Faith and deeds:** for Ahl al-Sunnah, deeds are not part of faith but show its maturity. The **Kharijites** and the **Mu'tazila** count deeds as part of faith.
- **Increase and decrease:** the Hanafi-Maturidi line holds that the essence of faith does not increase or decrease; Ash'aris and hadith scholars say it does.
- ***Taklidi*** (imitative) faith follows others; ***tahkiki*** (verified) faith rests on evidence.

## Articles of faith

- **Belief in God:** ***tevhid*** is God's oneness in essence, attributes and acts; its opposite is ***şirk***. Attributes are **essential** (existence, eternity without beginning, permanence, oneness...) and **affirmative** (life, knowledge, will, power...).
- **Angels:** beings created of light who obey God's command; Gabriel, Michael, Israfil, Azrael and the recording angels (*Kiramen Katibin*) are known.
- **Scriptures and prophets:** the Torah, Psalms, Gospel and Qur'an; prophets receive **revelation** and are supported by **miracles**.
- **Hereafter:** death and ***berzah*** (barzakh, the intermediate realm), the Last Day, resurrection, gathering, reckoning, the scale, paradise and hell.
- **Divine decree:** everything falls within God's knowledge and decree; human will and responsibility are not contrary to it.

## Commonly confused

- *Tasdiq* is affirming with the heart, *iqrar* declaring with the tongue.
- *Aqidah* is the articles of faith themselves; *kalam* is the discipline that explains and defends them with arguments.
