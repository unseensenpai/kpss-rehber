## Core idea

Intellectual disability (*zihin yetersizliği*) and autism spectrum disorder (*otizm spektrum bozukluğu*, OSB) are the two areas with the most students in special education, and with **5 questions** this is the heaviest subject in the test. Intellectual disability is defined by **intellectual functioning and adaptive behaviour**, ASD by **social communication and patterns of behaviour**. Questions usually describe a student and ask which diagnosis or practice fits.

## Intellectual disability

- **Definition:** significant limitations in intellectual functioning and in **adaptive behaviour** (conceptual, social and practical skills), beginning in the **developmental period**. A low IQ score alone is not enough for a diagnosis.
- **Classification:** mild, moderate, severe and profound; current approaches define the level by the **support needed**.
- **Causes:** prenatal (**Down syndrome**, fragile X, **phenylketonuria**), perinatal (lack of oxygen) and postnatal (infection, head injury, deprived environment).
- **Learning characteristics:** limited attention and short-term memory, **difficulty generalising**, slower learning, **learned helplessness** and outer-directedness.

## Autism spectrum disorder

- **Diagnostic criteria (DSM-5):** (1) persistent deficits in **social communication and interaction**; (2) **restricted, repetitive** behaviour, interests and activities (including over- or under-reactivity to sensory input). Symptoms appear in the early developmental period.
- **Early signs:** lack of **joint attention**, not responding to their name, reduced eye contact, not pointing.
- **Language features:** **echolalia** (repeating what is heard), pronoun reversal, difficulty with the social use of language (pragmatics).
- **Evidence-based practices:** teaching based on applied behaviour analysis, discrete trial teaching, video modelling, social stories, picture activity schedules, PECS. Practices such as facilitated communication have no scientific support.

## Commonly confused

- An *intellectual disability* diagnosis looks at adaptive behaviour **together with** intelligence; an *ASD* diagnosis is independent of IQ.
- *Echolalia* is repeating what is heard; *stereotypy* is purposeless, repetitive body movement.
- *Down syndrome* is chromosomal; *phenylketonuria* is metabolic, and a diet started early can prevent its effects.

## Study tip

Reduce each diagnosis to two criteria: "intellectual functioning + adaptive behaviour" for intellectual disability, "social communication + restricted repetitive behaviour" for ASD. Whichever pair the case fits is the answer.
