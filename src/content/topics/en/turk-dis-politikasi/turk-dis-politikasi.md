## Core idea

The Republic's foreign policy is summed up by Atatürk's principle **"Peace at home, peace in the world"** (*Yurtta sulh, cihanda sulh*): not territorial expansion but **protecting independence and the status quo**, staying committed to international law and peaceful means. In the Cold War this line continued through membership of the **Western alliance**. The test has **6 questions** on this subject; they usually ask about the period and result of a treaty or crisis.

## 1923-1939

- **Treaty of Lausanne (1923):** international recognition of the new state. Issues left open: **Mosul**, the **Straits**, the **population exchange**, foreign schools and debts.
- **Mosul:** taken to the League of Nations; left to Iraq by the **1926 Treaty of Ankara**.
- **League of Nations:** Turkey joined in **1932**.
- **Balkan Entente (1934)** and **Saadabad Pact (1937):** searches for regional security.
- **Montreux Convention on the Straits (1936):** abolished the international commission set up at Lausanne and recognised **Turkish sovereignty** over the Straits and the right to fortify them.
- **Hatay:** joined Turkey in **1939** through peaceful diplomacy.

## The Second World War and the Cold War

- **Turkey in the war:** despite the **1939 Anglo-French-Turkish alliance**, a policy of staying out of the war (**active neutrality**); declaring war on Germany and Japan at the end made Turkey a founding member of the UN.
- **Soviet demands (1945):** demands for bases on the Straits and for Kars-Ardahan pushed Turkey towards the West.
- **Western alliance:** **Truman Doctrine** and **Marshall Plan** aid, troops sent to the **Korean War** and **NATO membership in 1952**. The **Baghdad Pact (1955)** was later renamed CENTO.
- **Johnson Letter (1964):** a US warning during the Cyprus crisis; it started Turkey's search for a **multi-dimensional foreign policy**.

## Cyprus, the Aegean and Europe

- **Cyprus:** the independent Republic of Cyprus was founded in 1960, with Turkey, Greece and the UK as guarantors. In **1974**, citing its guarantor rights, Turkey carried out the **Cyprus Peace Operation**.
- **Aegean questions:** territorial waters, the continental shelf, airspace and FIR, and the militarisation of islands.
- **EU relations:** association with the EEC by the **Ankara Agreement (1963)**, a full membership application in **1987**, the **Customs Union in 1996**, candidacy at the **1999 Helsinki Summit** and accession negotiations from **2005**.

## The post-Cold War period

- New relations in the Caucasus, Central Asia and the Balkans; recognition of the Turkic republics.
- Security and energy issues arising from Iraq, Syria and the Eastern Mediterranean.
- Follow developments of recent years in current sources.

## Commonly confused

- The *Lausanne Straits Convention* set up an international commission; *Montreux* abolished it and gave authority to Turkey.
- The *Balkan Entente* aimed at securing the western borders, the *Saadabad Pact* the eastern ones.
- The *Ankara Agreement* created the association; the *Customs Union* was the move to its final stage.
