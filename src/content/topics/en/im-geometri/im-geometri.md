## Core idea

Geometry (*geometri*) starts from a few **axioms** and proves and measures the properties of figures. The Elementary Mathematics ÖABT has **12 questions** on it. Most ask you to compute an angle, length, area or volume; some use equations of lines and circles in the coordinate plane, and some ask about transformations such as translation, reflection and rotation. Every triangle, polygon, transformation and solid topic of the lower-secondary curriculum draws on this subject.

## Basic ideas and triangles

- **Euclid's fifth (parallel) postulate:** through a point not on a line exactly one parallel can be drawn. Changing this axiom leads to non-Euclidean geometries.
- **Angles:** the interior angles of a triangle sum to 180°; an exterior angle equals the sum of the two non-adjacent interior angles. An n-sided polygon has interior angles summing to (n − 2)·180°.
- **Triangle inequality:** |b − c| < a < b + c.
- **Congruence:** triangles are congruent if SAS, ASA or SSS (*KAK, AKA, KKK*) holds.
- **Similarity:** with similarity ratio k, perimeters scale by k, areas by k² and volumes by k³.
- **Relations:** in a right triangle a² + b² = c² (Pythagoras); in any triangle a² = b² + c² − 2bc·cos A (law of cosines). Area = ½·b·c·sin A.

## The circle

- An **inscribed angle** is half the central angle on the same arc; an inscribed angle on a diameter is 90°.
- A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact; the two tangent segments from an outside point are equal.
- A circle of radius r has circumference 2πr and area πr²; a sector of α degrees has area πr²·α/360.

## Analytic geometry

- **Distance and midpoint:** |AB| = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²), midpoint ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2).
- **Slope:** m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁). Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular lines satisfy m₁·m₂ = −1.
- **Distance from a point to a line:** from (x₀, y₀) to ax + by + c = 0 it is |ax₀ + by₀ + c| / √(a² + b²).
- **Circle equation:** centre (a, b) and radius r give (x − a)² + (y − b)² = r².

## Transformations and solids

- **Isometry:** a transformation that preserves length (translation, reflection, rotation). These are exactly the transformations you will teach in lower-secondary school.
- **In coordinates:** reflection in the x-axis (x, y) → (x, −y); in the line y = x (x, y) → (y, x); rotation by 90° about the origin (x, y) → (−y, x).
- **Volumes:** prism and cylinder are base area × height; pyramid and cone are one third of that; sphere (4/3)πr³, sphere surface 4πr².
- **Euler's formula:** for a convex polyhedron, vertices − edges + faces = 2.

## Commonly confused

- *Congruence* preserves both shape and size; *similarity* preserves only shape.
- An *inscribed angle* measures half its arc; a *central angle* measures the whole arc.
- *Translation* and *rotation* preserve orientation; *reflection* reverses it (clockwise becomes anticlockwise).

## Study tip

Start every question by drawing the figure and looking for similar or right triangles; once you find a right triangle with known sides, the question usually falls to Pythagoras or a similarity ratio.
