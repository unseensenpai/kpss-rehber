## Core idea

Worship (*ibadet*) expresses reverence for and devotion to God in set forms. Islamic law (*fıkıh*) gives every act a ruling for a **sane, adult** person (*mükellef*, legally responsible), and each act of worship has its own **conditions, obligatory elements (pillars)** and recommended practices. Questions are mostly of the "which of these is not an obligatory element of ablution?" type, or ask about a well-known difference between the *Hanefi* and *Şafii* (Shafi'i) schools.

## Legal rulings

- ***Farz*** (obligatory): required by definitive evidence; denying it is unbelief. *Farz-ı ayn* binds every individual (the five daily prayers); *farz-ı kifaye* binds the community and lapses for the rest once some perform it (the funeral prayer).
- ***Vacip*** (necessary): only in the Hanefi school, a separate level between farz and sünnet (the *vitir* prayer, the eid prayer). For the Şafiis, farz and vacip mean the same.
- ***Sünnet*** / *mendup* (recommended): what the Prophet did and encouraged; divided into *müekkede* (regularly practised) and *gayr-i müekkede*. ***Mübah*** (permissible) is left to free choice.
- ***Mekruh*** (disliked) should be avoided; ***haram*** (forbidden) is definitively prohibited. Hanefis divide mekruh into *tahrimen* (close to haram) and *tenzihen* (close to permissible).

## Ritual purity

- **Obligatory elements of *abdest* (ablution), Hanefi (four):** washing the face, washing the arms including the elbows, wiping a quarter of the head, washing the feet including the ankles. The Şafiis add **intention** (*niyet*) and **order** (*tertip*).
- **Obligatory elements of *gusül* (full bath), Hanefi (three):** rinsing the mouth (*mazmaza*), rinsing the nose (*istinşak*), washing the whole body. For the Şafiis they are intention and washing the whole body; mouth and nose are sunnah.
- ***Teyemmüm*** (dry ablution) is done with clean earth when water is unavailable or cannot be used; its obligatory elements are **intention** and **two strikes** (wiping the face and the arms).

## Prayer and fasting

- **Obligatory elements outside the prayer (conditions):** purity from ritual impurity (*hades*), purity from physical impurity (*necaset*), covering the body (*setr-i avret*), facing the qibla (*istikbal-i kıble*), the time, intention.
- **Obligatory elements inside the prayer (pillars):** the *opening takbir* (*iftitah tekbiri*), *kıyam* (standing), *kıraat* (recitation), bowing (*rükû*), prostration (*secde*), the *final sitting* (*ka'de-i ahire*). The Hanefis treat the opening takbir as a condition; the other schools treat it as a pillar.
- **Times:** dawn, noon, afternoon, sunset, night. **Vitir** is vacip for the Hanefis and sunnah for the Şafiis. The Friday prayer is obligatory for men.
- **Fasting:** fasting in Ramadan is farz. Its obligatory elements are **intention** and ***imsak*** (abstaining from food, drink and sexual relations from dawn to sunset). A fast broken for a valid excuse requires only ***kaza*** (making up the day); for the Hanefis, deliberately breaking a Ramadan fast requires ***kefaret*** (expiation: sixty consecutive days of fasting).

## Zakat, hajj and sacrifice

- **Conditions of *zekât* (zakat):** being Muslim, free, sane and adult; owning growing wealth at the ***nisap*** (minimum threshold) beyond basic needs and debts; one full year passing over it (*havelan-ı havl*). For the Şafiis, zakat is also due on the wealth of children and the mentally ill. The rate on money, gold and trade goods is **one fortieth**.
- **Recipients** are listed as eight groups in Sura al-Tawbah, verse 60: the poor, the destitute, zakat collectors, those whose hearts are to be won over, slaves, debtors, those in God's cause, travellers. One may not give zakat to one's **ascendants** (parents, grandparents), **descendants** (children, grandchildren) or spouse.
- ***Sadaka-i fıtır*** (*fitre*, the Ramadan charity) is given before the end-of-Ramadan festival; for the Hanefis it is vacip.
- **Hajj** is farz once in a lifetime for a Muslim who is able. For the Hanefis, ***ihram*** is a condition, while the **standing at Arafat** (*vakfe*) and the **visiting circumambulation** (*ziyaret tavafı*) are pillars. **Umrah** is sunnah for the Hanefis and farz for the Şafiis.
- ***Kurban*** (sacrifice) is offered on the days of the Festival of Sacrifice; it is vacip for those who can afford it according to the Hanefis and an emphasised sunnah according to the Şafiis.

## Commonly confused

- *Farz* rests on definitive evidence, *vacip* on evidence of lesser certainty; only the Hanefis make this distinction.
- *Kaza* only makes up the missed day; *kefaret* is an additional, penalty-like duty for deliberately breaking the fast.
- *Zekât* is a financial obligation tied to the threshold and a full year; *fitre* is a per-person charity tied to Ramadan.

## Study tip

Make a table of the obligatory elements of ablution, full bath, dry ablution, prayer and hajj, and note the Şafii difference beside each row. Questions very often test exactly these lists and school differences.
