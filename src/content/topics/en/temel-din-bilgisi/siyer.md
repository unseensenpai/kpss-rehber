## Core idea

*Siyer* (sirah) studies the life of the Prophet Muhammad and the formation of the Muslim community. It falls into two main periods: the **Meccan period**, of calling people to Islam and rooting the faith, and the **Medinan period**, of building a community and a state. Questions usually ask you to order events, match an event with its result, or tell the two periods apart. Rather than memorising dates one by one, build the chain of cause and effect between events.

## Before Islam and before prophethood

- **Cahiliye (jahiliyya, pre-Islamic era):** Arab society before Islam, marked by tribal loyalty, idol worship, blood feuds and harsh treatment of baby girls. There were also *hanifs*, who held to Abraham’s belief in one God.
- **Birth and childhood:** the Prophet was born in Mecca; his father Abdullah died before his birth. He was nursed by Halima, lost his mother Amina at a young age and was then protected by his grandfather Abd al-Muttalib and his uncle Abu Talib.
- **Youth:** he joined the **Hilfu’l-fudul** (league of the virtuous), formed to protect the wronged, and was known as “al-Amin” (the trustworthy) for his honesty. He married Khadija and settled the dispute over the Black Stone during the rebuilding of the Kaaba by arbitration.

## The Meccan period

- **First revelation:** came in 610, when he was forty, in the cave of Hira.
- **Secret call:** the first Muslims include Khadija, Abu Bakr, Ali and Zayd ibn Haritha. Muslims met and were taught at **Darü’l-Erkam** (the house of al-Arqam).
- **Open call:** first close relatives, then all Meccans were invited; pressure from the polytheists grew.
- **Migrations to Abyssinia:** a group of persecuted Muslims twice migrated to the land of the Negus (*Necaşi*), known for his justice.
- **Boycott and year of sorrow:** the Banu Hashim endured a boycott of about three years. The year in which Khadija and Abu Talib then died is called the year of sorrow.
- **Taif and Isra-Miraj:** the journey to invite the people of Taif was rejected. The Night Journey and Ascension (*İsra ve Miraç*) also took place in this difficult period.
- **Pledges of Aqaba:** two pledges with Muslims from Medina prepared the ground for the Hijra.

## The Hijra and the Medinan period

- **Hijra (622):** the Prophet migrated to Medina with Abu Bakr; the Quba Mosque was built on the way. The Hijri calendar takes this event as its starting point.
- **Building the community:** the Prophet’s Mosque was built, and **muahat** (brotherhood) was established between the Meccan emigrants (*muhacir*) and the Medinan helpers (*ensar*).
- **Constitution of Medina:** a written document setting out the rights and duties of Muslims, Jews and other groups.
- **Badr (624):** the first major battle, ending in a Muslim victory.
- **Uhud (625):** when the archers left their posts, the Muslims suffered heavy losses; Hamza was martyred.
- **Khandaq (627):** Medina was defended by digging a trench around it.
- **Hudaybiyya (628):** a treaty with the polytheists; the Meccans in effect recognised the Muslims as a party, and the message spread in peace.
- **Conquest of Mecca (630):** Mecca was taken without major fighting, the Kaaba was cleared of idols and a general amnesty was declared.
- **Farewell Pilgrimage (632):** the Prophet’s only pilgrimage; the Farewell Sermon stressed the inviolability of life, property and honour and the equality of people. He died the same year.

## Commonly confused

- The *migration to Abyssinia* was a temporary refuge in the Meccan period; the *Hijra to Medina* founded a new community.
- *Muahat* is the brotherhood between emigrants and helpers; the *Constitution of Medina* is a written agreement among the city’s groups.
- *Hudaybiyya* is a treaty, *Khandaq* a defensive battle; Khandaq came first.

## Study tip

Draw two separate timelines for the Meccan and Medinan periods and write a one-sentence result next to each event. For ordering questions, keeping the chain Badr, Uhud, Khandaq, Hudaybiyya, conquest of Mecca and Farewell Pilgrimage in mind solves most items.
