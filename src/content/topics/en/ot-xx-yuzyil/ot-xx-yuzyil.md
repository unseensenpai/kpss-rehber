## Core idea

The 20th century was shaped by two world wars and the **bipolar Cold War** order that followed; Turkey set its foreign policy according to these balances. The test has 5 questions; they usually ask about the results of the wars, Cold War events and Turkey's choices in this process.

## The wars and the interwar years

- **World War I (1914–1918):** the Central Powers and the Entente; **Wilson's Fourteen Points**, the **Paris Peace Conference**, **Versailles (1919)** and the **League of Nations**.
- The **1917 revolutions in Russia** brought the Bolsheviks to power and led to the USSR.
- The **Great Depression of 1929** strengthened state-led policies and totalitarian regimes: **fascism** in Italy and **Nazism** in Germany.
- **World War II (1939–1945):** began with Germany's attack on Poland; in 1941 the USSR and the USA entered the war. The **Yalta** and **Potsdam** conferences shaped the post-war order; the **United Nations (1945)** was founded.
- **Turkey** stayed neutral for most of the war; in 1945 it declared war on Germany and Japan and so became one of the founding members of the UN.

## The Cold War

- The **Truman Doctrine (1947)** and the **Marshall Plan** included Turkey and Greece; **NATO (1949)**, **Warsaw Pact (1955)**.
- Turkey sent troops to the **Korean War** and joined NATO in **1952**.
- The **Berlin Wall (1961)**, the **Cuban Missile Crisis (1962)**; then a period of **détente**.
- **Decolonisation:** independence across Asia and Africa; the **Bandung Conference (1955)** and the **Non-Aligned Movement**.
- **Cyprus:** the Republic of Cyprus was founded in 1960; in 1974 Turkey, as a guarantor power, carried out a military operation on the island.
- The **fall of the Berlin Wall (1989)** and the **dissolution of the USSR (1991)** ended the bipolar order.

## After the Cold War

- Turkey recognised the newly independent Turkic republics and developed ties with them; at the **1999 Helsinki Summit** it was accepted as an EU candidate.
- **Globalisation:** trade, communication and regional organisations gained weight; the **European Union** was created by the Maastricht Treaty.

## Commonly confused

- The *Truman Doctrine* was military and political aid; the *Marshall Plan* was economic aid.
- *Yalta* dealt with post-war spheres of influence; *Potsdam* with the administration of Germany.
- *Détente* is the easing of tension between the blocs; *non-alignment* is the choice to join neither bloc.

## Study tip

Write each Turkish foreign-policy step next to a world event of the same time (NATO membership and Korea, EU candidacy and the post-Cold War world). "Under the influence of which development" questions then become easier.
