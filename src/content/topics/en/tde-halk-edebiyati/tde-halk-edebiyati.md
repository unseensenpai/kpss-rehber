## Core idea

Folk literature is born in **oral culture**, passed from generation to generation and built on syllabic metre and the quatrain. The Turkish Language and Literature ÖABT has **10 questions** on it. The topic rests on three branches, **anonymous**, **minstrel** (*âşık*) and **religious-mystical** literature, plus narrative genres and traditional theatre. Questions ask you to identify a text's genre and tradition, know minstrel concepts and tell narrative genres apart.

## Epics and narrative genres

- **Epic (*destan*):** tells extraordinary events and heroes kept in a nation's memory. The pre-Islamic **Oğuz Kağan** narrative and the Kyrgyz epic **Manas** are well-known examples.
- ***Dede Korkut* stories:** depict the life, values and internal conflicts of the Oghuz tribes; seen as a bridge from epic to folk romance, mixing verse and prose.
- **Folk romance (*halk hikâyesi*):** love (*Kerem ile Aslı*, *Âşık Garip*) and heroic (*Köroğlu*) themes; long prose narratives with inserted poems, told by minstrels.
- **Folk tale (*masal*):** extraordinary, with no fixed time or place; opens with a **tekerleme** (formulaic rhyme). A **legend** (*efsane*) is believed and tied to a place or event.
- **Anecdote (*fıkra*):** a short, witty story; its best-known figure is Nasreddin Hoca.

## Anonymous poetry

- ***Mani*:** a single quatrain, seven syllables, rhymed *aaxa*.
- ***Türkü*:** a sung poem of verse sections and a refrain (*kavuştak*); named by its theme.
- **Lullaby, lament (*ağıt*), riddle, *tekerleme*, proverb and idiom.**

## Minstrel literature

- **Tradition:** the **master-apprentice relationship**, receiving a pen name, the motif of **drinking the love potion** (*bade*) in a dream, **poetic duels** (*atışma*) with the *saz*, riddles (*muamma*) and ***lebdeğmez*** (poems avoiding lip consonants).
- **Forms:** ***koşma*** (11 syllables; *güzelleme*, *koçaklama*, *taşlama*, *ağıt*), ***semai*** and ***varsağı*** (8 syllables), ***destan*** (a long poem narrating events).
- **Poets:** Karacaoğlan, Köroğlu, Dadaloğlu, Pir Sultan Abdal, Âşık Veysel.

## Religious-mystical literature and theatre

- **Lodge poetry:** *ilahi*, **nefes**, *nutuk*, *devriye*, *şathiye*. Ahmet Yesevi with his *hikmet* poems and **Yunus Emre** with his plain Turkish are the key names.
- **Traditional theatre:** **Karagöz** (shadow play with figures behind a screen; Karagöz and Hacivat), **orta oyunu** (open-air play with Kavuklu and Pişekâr), **meddah** (one-person storytelling).

## Commonly confused

- An *epic* is a community's shared heroic memory; a *folk romance* is a more individual tale of love and adventure told by minstrels.
- A *folk tale* makes no claim to truth; a *legend* is told as believed.
- *Karagöz* uses a screen and figures; *orta oyunu* uses live actors.
