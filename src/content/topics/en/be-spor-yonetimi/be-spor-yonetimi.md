## Core idea

Sports management (*spor yönetimi*) is the planned use of **people, facilities, equipment and time** so that physical education and sport activities reach their aims. A teacher organising lessons, school teams and tournaments is a small-scale manager too. The test has **2 questions**; they usually ask about management functions, sports organisations or tournament systems.

## Management functions

- **Planning:** setting goals and the way to reach them in advance.
- **Organising:** distributing tasks, authority and resources.
- **Directing and coordination:** getting people to act and bringing their efforts into line.
- **Control:** comparing results with goals and making corrections.

## Sports organisations

- **Türkiye:** the central body for sport services is the **Ministry of Youth and Sports** (*Gençlik ve Spor Bakanlığı*); individual sports are run by **sports federations**. The **Ministry of National Education** also has a role in organising school sport. Check the current structure and duties of these bodies in official sources.
- **International:** the **International Olympic Committee** leads the Olympic movement, and each country has a **national Olympic committee**. Individual sports are governed by international federations (FIFA for football, FIBA for basketball, FIVB for volleyball and so on).

## Organising tournaments

- **League (round-robin) system:** in a single round-robin with n teams there are **n × (n − 1) / 2** matches; every team plays a lot and the result is fairest, but it takes long.
- **Single knockout system:** the loser is out; there are **n − 1** matches. It is quick, but teams play few matches.
- **Bye:** if the number of teams is not a power of 2, some teams pass the first round without playing.
- **Facilities and safety:** equipment maintenance, checking the playing area, first aid readiness and risk assessment are part of management.

## Commonly confused

- *Planning* comes before the work starts; *control* happens during and after it.
- The *league system* is fair but long; the *knockout system* is quick but has few matches per team.

## Study tip

Test the tournament formulas with a small example (4 teams: league 6 matches, single knockout 3 matches). Even if you forget the formula, this gets you to the right answer.
