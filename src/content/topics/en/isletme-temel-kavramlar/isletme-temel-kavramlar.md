## Core idea

A business is an economic unit that brings together the **factors of production** to make goods and services that meet human needs. This subject sets out what a business is: why it is founded, how its success is measured, which legal form it takes and how it grows. The test has **4 questions**; they usually ask about the formulas of success measures, the features of company types and forms of combination.

## Factors of production and environment

- **Natural resources** (earning rent), **labour** (wages), **capital** (interest) and the **entrepreneur** (profit). The entrepreneur combines the other three and bears the risk.
- **Aims:** making a profit, survival, growth, serving society.
- **Environment:** the **internal environment** is the firm's own resources; the **external environment** consists of the general environment (economic, legal, technological, socio-cultural) and the task environment the firm deals with directly (customers, competitors, suppliers).

## Measures of success

- **Productivity** (*verimlilik*): output / input, measured in physical quantities.
- **Economy ratio** (*ekonomiklik*): monetary value of outputs / monetary value of inputs (sales revenue / total cost). Above 1 means the firm is profitable.
- **Profitability** (*kârlılık, rantabilite*): profit / capital employed; shows how much the capital earns.

## Types by legal form

- **Sole proprietorship** (*şahıs işletmesi*): one owner, unlimited liability.
- **Simple partnership** (*adi ortaklık*): no legal personality; formed by contract.
- **General partnership** (*kollektif şirket*): all partners are natural persons with **unlimited** liability for company debts.
- **Limited partnership** (*komandit şirket*): the *komandite* partner has unlimited liability, the *komanditer* partner is liable only up to the capital contributed.
- **Joint-stock company** (*anonim şirket*): capital is divided into shares; a shareholder is liable only for the capital pledged. It can be founded by a single shareholder.
- **Limited liability company** (*limited şirket*): 1 to 50 partners; liability limited to the capital share.
- **Cooperative** (*kooperatif*): protects members' common economic interests through mutual help; founded by at least 7 members.

## Location, capacity and growth

- **Location** is chosen by factors such as nearness to raw materials, market, labour, energy and transport.
- **Capacity:** **technical (maximum)** capacity is the highest possible output; at **optimal** capacity unit cost is lowest; the unused part is **idle capacity** (*atıl kapasite*).
- **Combinations:** in a **cartel** firms stay independent and agree to restrict competition. In a **trust** they lose independence and merge into one firm. A **holding** controls other companies by owning their shares. A **consortium** is a temporary union for a large project. A **joint venture** sets up a new firm.
- A **horizontal** merger joins firms at the same stage of production; a **vertical** merger joins firms at successive stages.

## Commonly confused

- *Productivity* uses physical quantities, the *economy ratio* uses monetary values.
- A *cartel* keeps independence; a *trust* removes it.
- The *komandite* partner has unlimited, the *komanditer* partner limited liability.
