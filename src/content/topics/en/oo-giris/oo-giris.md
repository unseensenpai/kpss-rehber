## Core idea

Early childhood education is systematic education that supports **all areas of development** from birth to primary school. Brain development is very fast in these years and the habits formed lay the foundation for later learning, so early education provides **equal opportunity**, especially for disadvantaged children. The test has 4 questions; they usually ask about thinkers' contributions, the field's principles and types of institutions.

## Principles

- **Holistic development:** cognitive, language, motor and social-emotional development and self-care are addressed together.
- **Child-centredness:** the child's interests, needs and developmental level come first; individual differences are respected.
- **Play-based learning:** play is the child's most natural way of learning.
- **Family involvement:** the family is a partner in education.
- **Concrete experience:** children learn through their senses and active participation.

## Thinkers

- **Comenius:** stressed the importance of the early years; produced *Orbis Pictus*, considered one of the first illustrated children's books.
- **Rousseau:** in *Emile* argued for natural education suited to the child's nature; criticised treating the child as a small adult.
- **Pestalozzi:** education of "head, heart and hand"; learning through the senses, from concrete to abstract.
- **Froebel:** founder of the **kindergarten**; put play at the centre of education and designed educational materials called "gifts".
- **Robert Owen:** opened an *infant school* for workers' children.
- **Montessori and Dewey:** influenced the field with the prepared environment and learning by doing (details under programmes and approaches).

## Types of institutions

- **Independent preschool** (*anaokulu*): a stand-alone preschool institution.
- **Preschool class** (*anasınıfı*): a class opened within a primary or other school.
- **Practice class** (*uygulama sınıfı*): a class in vocational schools where students do supervised practice.
- **Nurseries and day-care centres** (*kreş*): care and education for younger age groups.

## Commonly confused

- *Froebel* is linked to the kindergarten and play gifts, *Montessori* to the prepared environment and self-correcting materials.
- An *anaokulu* is an independent institution, an *anasınıfı* sits inside another school.
- *Rousseau* stands for natural education, *Pestalozzi* for the sensory education that put it into practice.

## Study tip

Memorise one keyword per thinker (Froebel: kindergarten, Pestalozzi: head-heart-hand, Comenius: Orbis Pictus). Questions usually describe that key idea in other words.
