## Core idea

Antiquity runs from the first use of writing to the fall of the Western Roman Empire (476). In this age the basic institutions of civilisation appeared: farming, cities, writing, law and the state. The test has 2 questions; they usually ask you to match a civilisation with its contribution and to tell Anatolian civilisations apart.

## Mesopotamia and Egypt

- **Sumerians:** **cuneiform** script, **ziggurat** temples, city-states and the Epic of Gilgamesh.
- **Akkadians:** the first large centralised state uniting the Mesopotamian city-states.
- **Babylon:** the **Code of Hammurabi**; retaliation in kind and different penalties by class.
- **Assyrians:** set up **trade colonies** in Anatolia (Kültepe-Kanesh); writing reached Anatolia this way.
- **Egypt:** **hieroglyphs**, papyrus, a calendar based on the solar year, pyramids and the god-king idea.

## Anatolian civilisations

- **Hittites:** capital **Hattusa**; the **Pankus** assembly, the active role of the queen (**Tawananna**), a polytheistic religion and annals. The **Treaty of Kadesh** with Egypt is among the oldest known written treaties.
- **Phrygians:** capital **Gordion**; they valued farming and treated harming a field as a grave crime.
- **Lydians:** capital **Sardis**; among the first peoples to use coined money.
- **Urartians:** capital **Tushpa** (Van); canals, dams and mining.
- **Ionians:** philosophy and science grew on the western Anatolian coast, with thinkers such as Thales.

## Other civilisations

- The **Phoenicians** spread the phonetic alphabet; the **Hebrews** developed monotheistic belief.
- The **Persians** divided their empire into **satrapies** and built the **Royal Road** from Sardis to Susa.
- The **Greeks** built the **polis** (city-state); Athenian democracy and the Spartan military order stand out. **Alexander the Great**'s conquests opened the **Hellenistic age**.
- **Rome** passed through kingdom, republic and empire; it split in two in **395**.

## Commonly confused

- The *Hittites* learned writing through the Assyrian colonies; the *Lydians* used coins.
- *Cuneiform* belongs to Mesopotamia; *hieroglyphs* to Egypt.
- The *Royal Road* is Persian; the *trade colonies* are Assyrian.

## Study tip

Make a "capital plus one contribution" card for each civilisation; almost all of this two-question section rests on that matching.
