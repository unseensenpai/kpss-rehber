## Core idea

In social studies teaching, history covers a wide span **from antiquity to the present**, but it asks for **the main idea of each period and the links between them** rather than depth. In every period think of political events, state organisation and culture together. The test has 22 questions; they usually ask about matching events with periods, cause and effect, and interpreting sources.

## The science of history and antiquity

- **Science of history:** sources (primary and secondary), chronology, calendars and historical thinking skills (using evidence, change and continuity, empathy).
- **Mesopotamia:** **cuneiform** among the Sumerians, the **Code of Hammurabi** in Babylon; hieroglyphs and the solar calendar in **Egypt**.
- **Anatolia:** the **Hittites** (Treaty of Kadesh), **Phrygians** (farming), **Lydians** (coinage), **Urartians** (canals), **Ionians** (philosophy).

## Turkic and Islamic history

- **Early Turkic states:** the Asian Huns (decimal system), the Göktürks (**Orkhon Inscriptions**), the Uyghurs (settled life); the concepts of **kut**, **töre** (customary law) and **kurultay** (council).
- **Islamic history:** the **Hijra (622)**, the four caliphs, the Umayyads and Abbasids; **Talas (751)** sped up the Turks' conversion to Islam.
- **Turkic-Islamic states:** the **Karakhanids** (first Muslim Turkic state), the **Ghaznavids**, the **Great Seljuks** (**Manzikert 1071**, the **iqta**, Nizamiyya madrasas).
- **Anatolian Seljuks and beyliks:** Turkish settlement in Anatolia, caravanserais, Mongol influence after **Kösedağ (1243)** and the age of the beyliks (principalities).

## Ottoman history

- **Founding and rise:** Bursa, the crossing into Rumelia, the **conquest of Constantinople (1453)**, the conquests of Selim I and Süleyman.
- **Organisation:** the **Imperial Council**, the **timar**, the **devshirme**, the millet system.
- **Change and reform:** **Karlowitz (1699)**, the Tulip Era, the reforms of Selim III and Mahmud II, the **Tanzimat (1839)**, the **Reform Edict (1856)**, the **Kanun-i Esasi (1876)**.
- **Dissolution:** the Treaty of Berlin, the Second Constitutional Era, the Tripolitanian and Balkan Wars, World War I.

## National Struggle, Republic and contemporary history

- **National Struggle:** Mudros, the Amasya Circular, the Erzurum and Sivas congresses, the **National Pact**, the **Grand National Assembly (1920)**, Sakarya and the Great Offensive, **Lausanne (1923)**.
- **Reforms:** proclamation of the Republic, abolition of the caliphate, the **Law on Unification of Education**, the Civil Code, the Alphabet Reform; **Atatürk's principles**.
- **Contemporary world:** the two world wars, the **Cold War**, Turkey's NATO membership (1952), the dissolution of the USSR (1991) and globalisation.

## Commonly confused

- The *Karakhanids* were the first Muslim Turkic state; the *Göktürks* were the first to use the name "Türk".
- The *Tanzimat Edict* gave guarantees to all subjects; the *Reform Edict* gave extra rights to non-Muslims.
- The *National Pact* set the aims of the National Struggle; *Lausanne* won their international recognition.

## Study tip

Make a "key event + institution + cultural work" card for each period. Across this wide 22-question field, linked revision covers more ground than deep study of a single period.
