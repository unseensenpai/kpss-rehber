## Core idea

Professional ethics are the principles and rules that set out how a counsellor should act to protect the client's welfare. In Türkiye the professional association's code of ethics and the Ministry of National Education's regulation on guidance services form the basic framework; check the current texts in official sources. The test has 2 questions; they usually present a dilemma and ask for the right behaviour.

## Basic principles

- **Autonomy:** respecting the client's right to make their own decisions.
- **Beneficence** and **non-maleficence:** promoting the client's well-being and avoiding practices that would harm them.
- **Justice:** serving everyone equally and without discrimination.
- **Fidelity:** keeping promises and being trustworthy.

## Ethics in practice

- **Confidentiality:** client information is not shared without consent.
- **Limits of confidentiality:** serious and imminent danger to the client or others, suspected abuse and legal requirements; these limits are explained at the start.
- **Informed consent:** informing about the process, aims, limits and rights; with minors, parents' rights and the child's best interests are considered together.
- **Dual relationships:** no relationship with a client outside counselling (business, romantic, close friendship).
- **Professional competence:** counsellors work only in areas they are trained for and **refer** when they are not competent.
- **Records:** kept accurately and stored securely.
- **Suspected abuse:** as a public employee the counsellor has a duty to report; follow school procedures and current legislation.

## Commonly confused

- *Confidentiality* is the counsellor's duty; the *limits of confidentiality* show when that duty is lifted.
- *Referral* is not abandoning the client but protecting their welfare by directing them to a suitable specialist.

## Study tip

In dilemma questions first check whether there is a safety risk; if not, prefer the option that protects the client's autonomy and confidentiality.
