## Core idea

The family is the child's first and most influential learning environment; what is learned at school does not last unless it is supported at home. Parent education aims to give families knowledge and skills about child development and communication, while family involvement aims for **partnership** between school and home. The test has 3 questions; they usually ask about parenting styles, communication techniques and involvement methods.

## Parenting styles

- **Authoritative** (*demokratik*): warmth together with clear limits; reasons for rules are explained.
- **Authoritarian** (*otoriter*): strict rules, low warmth, expectation of unquestioning obedience.
- **Permissive** (*izin verici*): warm but sets no limits.
- **Neglectful** (*ilgisiz*): both warmth and control are low.
- **Overprotective:** doing things for the child, blocking autonomy.

## Approaches

- **Gordon (Parent Effectiveness Training):** **active listening**, the **I-message** that expresses feelings without blame, and the **no-lose method** in which both sides win.
- **Adlerian approach (Dreikurs):** understanding the goal of misbehaviour; **natural and logical consequences** and encouragement instead of punishment.
- **Behavioural approach:** reinforcement, modelling, consistent rules.
- Mother-child education programmes run in Türkiye by civil society together with the Ministry of National Education are also examples of this field.

## Family involvement

- **Epstein's typology:** parenting support, communicating, volunteering, learning at home, decision-making, collaborating with the community.
- **Methods:** individual meetings, **home visits**, parent meetings, newsletters and notes, family participation activities in class, activities sent home.

## Commonly confused

- In the *authoritative* style there are limits but they are explained; in the *permissive* style there is warmth but no limits.
- A *natural consequence* follows from the behaviour by itself (no coat, feels cold); a *logical consequence* is arranged by the adult and related to the behaviour.

## Study tip

Place the styles in four boxes along the "warmth" and "control" axes; finding where the parent in the question sits on these two dimensions gives the answer.
