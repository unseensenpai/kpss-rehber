## Core idea

This section tests the **scientifically correct** basis of the science taught in primary school. The Primary School Teaching ÖABT has **5 questions** on it. The logic is that children learn science through everyday experience, but the teacher must know the correct explanation behind it and spot **misconceptions**. Questions usually ask you to explain an event or separate the variables in an experiment.

## Living things

- **Features of living things:** nutrition, respiration, growth, reproduction, excretion and response to stimuli.
- **Plants:** root, stem, leaf, flower; making food by **photosynthesis**.
- **Ecosystem:** producers, consumers, decomposers; the **food chain**.
- **Human body:** basic functions of the skeletal-muscular, digestive, circulatory and respiratory systems; healthy eating.

## Matter and physical events

- **Matter:** anything with mass and volume; solid, liquid and gas states. **Melting**, **freezing**, **evaporation** and **condensation** involve heat exchange.
- **Force:** pushes and pulls; they start, stop or redirect motion or change shape. **Friction** opposes motion; magnets attract some metals.
- **Light and sound:** light travels in straight lines, cannot pass through opaque matter and forms shadows; sound comes from vibration and needs a **material medium** to travel.
- **Electricity:** a **simple circuit** of battery, bulb, wires and switch; the bulb lights when the circuit is complete.

## Earth and the universe

- Earth's rotation on its axis causes **day and night**; its orbit around the Sun together with its axial tilt causes the **seasons**.
- The Moon is Earth's satellite; its phases come from the changing part of its sunlit side that we can see.

## Science process skills

- Observing, classifying, measuring, predicting, **forming hypotheses**, experimenting and drawing conclusions.
- **Variables:** independent (changed), dependent (measured) and controlled (kept constant).

## Commonly confused

- *Evaporation* happens at the surface at any temperature; *boiling* happens throughout the liquid at a set temperature.
- The *seasons* are caused by Earth's axial tilt, not its distance from the Sun.

## Study tip

For each topic, write down a common misconception children hold and its correct explanation. Questions often use these misconceptions as distractors.
