## Core idea

Assessment in special education (*özel eğitimde değerlendirme*) focuses less on ranking a student against others and more on showing **what they can and cannot do** and on guiding instruction. The process begins with screening, continues with diagnosis and placement, and goes on with monitoring throughout teaching. There are **3 questions**; they ask about the purposes of assessment, types of measurement and skill assessment methods.

## Purposes and stages

- **Screening:** identifying students at risk with short tools; it does not diagnose.
- **Diagnosis and placement:** deciding whether a disability exists, its type and the suitable educational setting; in Türkiye **educational assessment and diagnosis** is carried out at Guidance and Research Centres (*Rehberlik ve Araştırma Merkezi*, RAM).
- **Instructional planning:** determining the present level of performance for the IEP.
- **Monitoring and programme evaluation:** measuring progress regularly.

## Formal and informal assessment

- **Formal (standardised) tools:** administration and scoring are standard; most are **norm-referenced**, comparing the student with a reference group.
- **Informal tools:** teacher-made **criterion-referenced** tools, observation, interviews, error analysis, **portfolios**.
- **Criterion-referenced measurement:** compares performance with a predefined criterion; the most useful type for the IEP.

## Multi-step skills

- The skill is first broken into steps through **task analysis**.
- **Single opportunity method:** the student is stopped at the first incorrect or missing step; later steps are scored as not done.
- **Multiple opportunity method:** when the student cannot do a step, the teacher completes it and assessment continues with the next steps; every step is seen separately.

## Commonly confused

- *Norm-referenced* measurement compares the student with a group; *criterion-referenced* measurement compares them with a success criterion.
- The *single opportunity* method is quick but shows nothing after the first error; the *multiple opportunity* method gives information on every step.
- *Screening* opens the door; *diagnosis* makes the decision.

## Study tip

In every assessment question first find the purpose (screening, diagnosis or instructional planning); the right tool depends on it.
