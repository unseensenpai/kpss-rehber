## Core idea

The common thread in special education policy is that an individual with special educational needs is educated **with their peers as far as possible**, with support suited to their needs. In Türkiye the basic framework is set by **Decree-Law No. 573 on Special Education** (*573 sayılı Özel Eğitim Hakkında Kanun Hükmünde Kararname*) and the **Special Education Services Regulation** (*Özel Eğitim Hizmetleri Yönetmeliği*). There are **3 questions**; they ask about principles, settings and the RAM process. Legislation can change, so check details in the current texts on mevzuat.gov.tr and from the Ministry of National Education.

## Basic principles

- Special education should **start early**.
- Education is planned according to **individual needs**; the tool for this is the **IEP** (*BEP*).
- **Education with peers** and the least restrictive environment.
- **Family participation:** the family takes part at every stage of the process.
- Education continues without interruption and aims at the person's participation in society.

## Inclusion and settings

- **Inclusion / integration** (*kaynaştırma / bütünleştirme*): the student learns in a general education class alongside peers with the necessary support; it can be **full-time** or **part-time**.
- **Least restrictive environment:** the student is placed in the least separating setting in which their needs can be met; a separate setting is chosen only when necessary.
- **Settings (roughly in order of increasing restriction):** general class with support → **resource room** (*destek eğitim odası*) → special education class → special education school; plus **home-based** and **hospital** education.

## The role of RAM

- The **Guidance and Research Centre** (*Rehberlik ve Araştırma Merkezi*, RAM) carries out **educational assessment and diagnosis** and decides on referral to a suitable educational setting.
- The school prepares and implements the IEP and monitors the student's progress.

## Commonly confused

- *Inclusion* is the student joining the general class; the *resource room* is where that student gets extra support for set hours.
- *RAM* diagnoses and refers; the school's *IEP development team* prepares the programme.
- *Medical diagnosis* (hospital) and *educational diagnosis* (RAM) are different processes.

## Study tip

In placement questions ask "Can the student's needs be met in a less restrictive setting?"; if yes, the more restrictive option is wrong.
