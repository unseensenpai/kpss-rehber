## Core idea

A constitution is the **highest legal norm**, setting out the basic organisation of the state and the individual's rights against it. The subject covers constitutional theory, Turkish constitutional history and the current **1982 Constitution**. The Public Administration test has about **6 questions** on it. Questions mostly test the powers of institutions, comparisons of constitutions and the system introduced by the 2017 amendment.

## Concepts and constitutional history

- **Constituent power** (*kurucu iktidar*): the original power that makes a constitution; **derived constituent power** amends an existing one.
- **Rigid constitution:** harder to amend than an ordinary law; the 1982 Constitution is rigid.
- ***Kanun-ı Esasi* (1876):** the first Ottoman constitution; introduced constitutional monarchy and a two-chamber parliament.
- ***Teşkilat-ı Esasiye* (1921):** adopted national sovereignty and the **assembly government** system.
- **1924 Constitution:** the first constitution of the Republic; the fusion of powers continued.
- **1961 Constitution:** created the Constitutional Court, broad freedoms and a two-chamber structure.

## The 1982 Constitution: principles and rights

- **Unamendable provisions:** the first three articles (form of the state, characteristics of the Republic, integrity, language, flag, anthem, capital) cannot be amended, nor can an amendment be proposed.
- **Characteristics of the Republic:** a democratic, secular and social state governed by the **rule of law**.
- **Restricting rights:** only **by law**, only on grounds stated in the Constitution, without touching the **essence** of the right and in line with **proportionality**.
- In a **state of emergency** the exercise of rights may be suspended, but core rights such as the right to life cannot be touched.

## Legislature, executive and judiciary

- **Grand National Assembly (TBMM):** 600 members; elections are held every five years together with the presidential election.
- **President:** since the 2017 amendment the executive power belongs to the president alone; the **Council of Ministers** and the office of prime minister were abolished. Elected by the people; a person may be elected at most twice.
- **Presidential decree** (*Cumhurbaşkanlığı kararnamesi*): issued on matters of executive power; cannot be issued on matters explicitly regulated by law.
- **Constitutional Court:** reviews the constitutionality of laws, hears **individual applications** and sits as the Supreme Court (*Yüce Divan*).
- **Council of Judges and Prosecutors (HSK):** handles appointments and personnel matters of judges and prosecutors.

## Amending the Constitution

- A proposal needs at least **one third** of the total number of members.
- An amendment passed by **three fifths** goes to a referendum; one passed by **two thirds** may be promulgated by the president or put to a referendum.

## Commonly confused

- *Original constituent power* makes a new constitution; *derived constituent power* amends the existing one.
- The *Teşkilat-ı Esasiye* adopted assembly government; the *1924 Constitution* kept the fusion of powers with parliamentary elements.
- The *Constitutional Court* reviews norms; the *Council of State* (*Danıştay*) is the highest administrative court.

## Study tip

Compare the four Republican constitutions in one table with columns for sovereignty, structure of parliament, executive and constitutional review. Since the post-2017 framework changes often, always check the current constitutional text and legislation.
