## Core idea

This subject studies how solids and fluids behave under forces and how matter relates to heat. There are **5** questions in the test. They are mostly short calculations on pressure and buoyancy, Bernoulli, calorimetry and the first law of thermodynamics.

## Elasticity and fluids

- **Hooke's law:** within the elastic limit, stress is proportional to strain; Young's modulus Y = (F/A)/(ΔL/L).
- **Hydrostatic pressure:** P = P₀ + ρ·g·h; it grows with depth and does not depend on the container's shape.
- **Pascal's principle:** pressure applied to an enclosed liquid is transmitted equally in all directions (hydraulic press).
- **Archimedes' principle:** buoyant force F = ρ_liquid·g·V_submerged.
- **Continuity:** A₁·v₁ = A₂·v₂; flow speeds up where the pipe narrows.
- **Bernoulli:** P + ½·ρ·v² + ρ·g·h = constant; where speed rises, pressure falls.

## Heat and thermodynamics

- **Heat exchange:** Q = m·c·ΔT; during a phase change Q = m·L and temperature stays constant.
- **Expansion:** ΔL = L₀·α·ΔT; the volume expansion coefficient is about 3α.
- **Heat transfer:** conduction, convection and radiation.
- **Ideal gas:** PV = nRT; mean translational kinetic energy is (3/2)·k·T, so it is proportional to absolute temperature.
- **First law:** ΔU = Q − W (W is work done by the gas). For an isothermal ideal gas ΔU = 0; for an adiabatic process Q = 0.
- **Second law:** entropy of an isolated system does not decrease. Carnot efficiency η = 1 − T_cold/T_hot (in kelvin).

## Commonly confused

- *Heat* (*ısı*) is energy in transfer; *temperature* (*sıcaklık*) measures the mean kinetic energy per particle.
- *Specific heat* is used for a temperature change, *latent heat* for a phase change at constant temperature.

## Study tip

In thermodynamics questions write the sign convention down first and always convert temperatures to kelvin.
