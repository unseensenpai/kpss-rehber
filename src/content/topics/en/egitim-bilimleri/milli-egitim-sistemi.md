## Core idea

Under the **Basic Law of National Education No. 1739**, the Turkish national education system has two main parts: **formal education** and **non-formal education**. Formal education covers the school levels; non-formal education covers all education outside or alongside school. Questions usually ask you to match an institution or activity with the right part and level, and to know the rules on compulsory education.

## Legal foundations

- **Constitution (Art. 42):** no one may be deprived of the right to education; primary education is compulsory and free in state schools.
- **Basic Law of National Education No. 1739 (1973):** aims, principles and the general structure of the system.
- **Primary Education and Education Law No. 222 (1961):** how primary education works and is made compulsory.
- **Teaching Profession Law No. 7528 (2024):** selecting, training and the careers of teachers.

## Formal education

- **Pre-school education:** covers children below compulsory school age and is **optional**. It can be set up as independent kindergartens or as nursery classes attached to schools.
- **Primary education:** covers children aged **6–14** and is compulsory. Children who are **72 months** old by **31 December** of that year enrol in the first grade of primary school. Primary and lower secondary schools are set up as **independent schools** in principle.
- **Upper secondary education:** general, vocational and technical institutions and vocational education centres giving **four years of compulsory** formal or non-formal education after primary education.
- **Higher education:** at least two years of education after upper secondary; regulated by the Council of Higher Education (YÖK).
- Together, primary school, lower secondary school and upper secondary education make **12 years of compulsory education**.

## Non-formal education

- Covers all education organised alongside or outside formal education: courses at public education centres, literacy courses, vocational courses and lifelong learning activities.

## Structure of the Ministry

- **Central organisation:** the minister and the ministry's service units. The **Board of Education** (*Talim ve Terbiye Kurulu*) is the scientific advisory and decision-making body that reviews and approves curricula and textbooks.
- **National Education Council** (*Millî Eğitim Şûrası*): the ministry's highest advisory council.
- **Provincial organisation:** provincial and district directorates of national education.
- **National Education Academy:** established under Law No. 7528, attached to the ministry; runs the preparatory training of teacher candidates and teachers' professional development.

## Commonly confused

- *Pre-school* is optional; *primary* and *upper secondary* education are compulsory.
- *Formal* education is organised by age and level; *non-formal* education is open to everyone with no age limit.
- The *Board of Education* deals with curricula; the *National Education Council* is the highest advisory council.

## Study tip

Draw the system as a tree: two main branches at the root, four levels on the formal branch, and the age and compulsory status beside each level. Studying it together with the legislation topics reinforces the same facts from two sides.
