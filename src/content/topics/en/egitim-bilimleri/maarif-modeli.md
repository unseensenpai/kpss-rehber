## Core idea

The Türkiye Century Education Model (*Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli*) is the **holistic** model with which the Ministry of National Education renewed its curricula. The common text sets the goal of raising a **"competent and virtuous person"** and sums it up as "From Roots to the Future": preserving national and spiritual values while giving students the knowledge and skills of the age. The key is that knowledge, skills, dispositions and values are treated not separately but **as an interlinked web**.

## Core approach and student profile

- **Holistic education:** students' intellectual, social-emotional, physical and moral development are handled together.
- The **student profile** has four dimensions: **ontological integrity**, **epistemological integrity** (using knowledge in life, wisdom), **temporal integrity** (education from past to future) and **axiological maturity** (moral awareness and aesthetic outlook). Axiological maturity is the basis of the Virtue-Value-Action Framework.

## Learning outcomes framework

- **Conceptual skills:** basic skills, integrated skills and higher-order thinking skills, three intertwined dimensions.
- **Physical skills.**
- **Dispositions:** tendencies to put skills into action; **self**, **social** and **intellectual** dispositions.
- **Subject skills:** skills specific to each subject (e.g. mathematical reasoning).
- Conceptual and subject skills combine with the subject's **content framework** to form **learning outcomes**, which are shown step by step through the skill's **process components**.

## Cross-curricular components

- **Social-emotional learning skills:** not separate content but a skill set that runs through every curriculum.
- **Virtue-Value-Action Framework:** aims to move from actions to values, from values to a virtuous person, and finally to **"a peaceful family and society"** and **"a peaceful person in a liveable environment"**.
  - **Roof values:** **respect, responsibility and justice**.
  - **The person (personal life):** thrift, patience, privacy, modesty, healthy living, diligence.
  - **Family and social environment:** love, friendship, freedom, honesty, patriotism, helpfulness, family unity.
  - **Physical environment:** cleanliness, sensitivity, aesthetics, compassion.
- **Systems thinking and literacy skills:** information, digital, financial, visual, cultural, citizenship, data, sustainability and art literacy. They are not written into learning outcomes but taught **implicitly**, in a spiral from pre-school onwards.

## Evidence of learning and teaching-learning experiences

- **Evidence of learning (assessment):** continuous, **formative** assessment is the norm; assessment gives feedback and focuses on skills.
- **Teaching-learning experiences:** basic assumptions (prior learning), the **pre-assessment process**, **bridging** (linking new learning to earlier learning and to life) and teaching-learning practices.

## Differentiation and school-based planning

- **Differentiation:** flexible teaching suited to each student's interests, abilities and needs. **Enrichment** is for students ahead of their peers; **support** provides content, process and environment suited to a student's individual needs and pace.
- **School-based planning:** time set aside, by decision of the **subject teachers' board**, for research, projects, local studies, reading and similar work; it gives teachers flexibility to adapt the curriculum.
- **Activities outside the curriculum:** a social responsibility programme and lifelong learning. The model also includes a **comprehensive developmental guidance programme** and **teacher reflections**.

## Commonly confused

- *Conceptual skills* are shared across subjects; *subject skills* are specific to one.
- There are three *roof values*: respect, responsibility, justice.
- *Enrichment* is for students ahead; *support* is for students who need help.

## Study tip

Write the values in a four-box chart (roof, person, family and social environment, physical environment); value-domain matching questions are solved with it. The common text can be updated, so check the Ministry's current version before the exam.
