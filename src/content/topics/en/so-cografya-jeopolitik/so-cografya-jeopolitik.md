## Core idea

This section tests the knowledge of **Turkey's geography** that a primary teacher uses in social studies and life sciences. The Primary School Teaching ÖABT has **2 questions** on it. The logic is cause and effect: **location** shapes climate, climate shapes vegetation and farming, and together they shape population and settlement. Questions usually ask you to interpret this chain through an example.

## Location and landforms

- **Absolute location:** in the Northern Hemisphere, in the temperate belt; so the four seasons are distinct and the sun's rays are never vertical.
- **Relative location:** a bridge between Asia and Europe; surrounded by sea on three sides; the **Straits** link the Black Sea to the Mediterranean.
- **Landforms:** high average elevation; mountains parallel to the coast in the north and south, plateaus and plains inland.

## Climate and vegetation

- **Mediterranean climate:** hot, dry summers and mild, rainy winters; vegetation is **maquis** (shrubland).
- **Black Sea climate:** rain in every season; vegetation is **forest**.
- **Continental climate:** hot, dry summers and cold winters; **steppe** in the interior.

## Population, economy and geopolitics

- **Population:** dense on the coasts, in industrial centres and fertile plains; sparse in the high, rugged east.
- **Economy:** farming, herding, industry, tourism and transport; climatic variety diversifies crops.
- **Geopolitical importance:** a bridge between continents, the Straits, its position on energy transit routes and its closeness to the Middle East, the Caucasus and the Balkans.

## Commonly confused

- *Absolute location* concerns latitude and longitude; *relative location* concerns relations with surroundings.
- *Maquis* is the vegetation of the Mediterranean climate, *steppe* of the continental climate.

## Study tip

In every question, build the chain "location → climate → vegetation → life". Knowing one link usually lets you infer the others.
