## Core idea

Physics explains matter and energy with a handful of **fundamental laws**: Newton's laws govern motion, conservation principles govern energy, and charge and field concepts govern electricity. With **16 questions** it is the heaviest subject in the Science test and surveys the field at general-science level. Questions usually ask for a short calculation with a formula, the reading of a graph, or the matching of an everyday event to the right principle.

## Mechanics

- **Vector and scalar:** force, velocity, acceleration and displacement are vectors; mass, time, energy and speed are scalars.
- **Uniformly accelerated motion:** v = v₀ + a·t, x = v₀·t + ½·a·t², v² = v₀² + 2·a·x. The slope of a velocity-time graph gives acceleration; the area under it gives displacement.
- **Newton's laws:** inertia; F = m·a; action-reaction forces are equal in size, opposite in direction and act on **different bodies**.
- **Weight:** G = m·g (g ≈ 9.8 m/s² at Earth's surface). **Friction:** f = k·N.
- **Work and energy:** W = F·x·cosθ; Eₖ = ½·m·v², Eₚ = m·g·h; without friction, mechanical energy is conserved. **Power:** P = W/t (watt).
- **Momentum:** p = m·v; with no external force, total momentum is conserved. Impulse F·Δt = Δp.
- **Pressure:** for solids P = F/A, for liquids P = d·g·h. **Archimedes' principle:** the buoyant force equals the weight of liquid displaced by the submerged volume (F = V·d·g).
- **Simple machines:** they never save work; a gain in force is paid for in distance.

## Heat and temperature

- **Heat** is transferred energy (joule); **temperature** measures the average kinetic energy of particles. T(K) = t(°C) + 273.15.
- **Specific heat:** Q = m·c·ΔT; water's specific heat is about 4.18 J/(g·°C).
- **Change of state:** Q = m·L; a pure substance's temperature stays constant during a change of state.
- **Heat transfer:** conduction, convection (in fluids), radiation (works even in a vacuum).

## Electricity and magnetism

- **Coulomb's law:** F = k·q₁·q₂/r²; like charges repel, unlike charges attract.
- **Ohm's law:** V = I·R. In series, resistances add and current is the same everywhere; in parallel, voltage is the same and 1/Rₑ = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂.
- **Electrical power:** P = V·I = I²·R.
- **Magnetism:** a current-carrying wire creates a magnetic field around it; a changing magnetic flux induces a current (**induction**, the generator principle).

## Waves, optics and modern physics

- **Wave equation:** v = λ·f. Sound is a mechanical, longitudinal wave and cannot travel through a vacuum; light is a transverse electromagnetic wave travelling at about 3×10⁸ m/s in a vacuum.
- **Reflection:** angle of incidence equals angle of reflection. **Refraction:** light passing into an optically denser medium bends toward the normal (n₁·sinθ₁ = n₂·sinθ₂).
- **Lenses:** a converging (convex) lens gathers light, a diverging (concave) lens spreads it.
- **Photoelectric effect:** shows the particle (photon) nature of light; photon energy E = h·f.

## Commonly confused

- *Mass* is the amount of matter and does not change from place to place; *weight* is a force and changes with g.
- *Heat* is transferred energy; *temperature* indicates average kinetic energy.
- *Action-reaction* forces act on different bodies; *balanced forces* act on the same body.

## Study tip

Write every formula with its units and check answers by unit analysis; an option with the wrong units can often be eliminated at once. In graph questions, first decide what the slope and the area represent.
