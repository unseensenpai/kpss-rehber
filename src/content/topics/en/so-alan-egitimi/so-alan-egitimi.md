## Core idea

Field teaching tests **how** a primary teacher teaches their subjects: first reading and writing, Turkish, mathematics, science, life sciences (*hayat bilgisi*) and social studies. It is the heaviest part of the Primary School Teaching ÖABT: **33** of the 50 questions come from here. The logic is that primary children learn by starting from **concrete experience**. Current curricula are organised under the **Türkiye Century Education Model**; for the model's general framework see the [Maarif Model](/kpss-rehber/en/topic/egitim-bilimleri/maarif-modeli/) topic. Questions mostly describe a classroom scene and ask what the teacher did or should do.

## First reading and writing, and Turkish

- **Sound-based sentence method:** moves from sounds to syllables, words and sentences; reading and writing are taught together.
- **Process:** preparation (listening, **phonological awareness**, fine-motor and line exercises), transition to first reading and writing (sensing, recognising, reading and writing a sound, building syllables from sounds), forming texts and independent reading.
- **Reading fluency:** accuracy, suitable speed and **prosody** (stress, intonation); supported by repeated reading and paired reading.
- **Reading difficulties:** errors such as omission, addition, reversal and repetition; individual support based on reading error analysis.
- **Comprehension:** predicting before reading, questioning during reading, summarising after; planning, drafting and revising in writing.

## Teaching mathematics

- **Concrete to abstract:** first concrete materials (base-ten blocks, fraction strips), then visual models, finally symbols. This order rests on Bruner's **enactive, iconic and symbolic** stages of representation.
- **Geometry:** by the **van Hiele** levels, a child first sees a shape as a whole, then notices its properties, later relates properties to one another.
- **Problem solving:** **Polya**'s four steps: understand the problem, devise a plan, carry out the plan, look back.
- **Misconceptions:** for example "multiplication always makes a number bigger" or "a fraction with a bigger denominator is bigger". A pupil's error is a diagnostic tool.

## Teaching science

- **Inquiry-based learning:** pupils ask questions, predict, test and give evidence-based explanations.
- **5E model:** engage, explore, explain, elaborate, evaluate.
- **Conceptual change:** first bring out the pupil's prior ideas, then present an observation that contradicts them.
- Science process skills and the **nature of science** (knowledge rests on evidence and can change).

## Life sciences and social studies

- **Life sciences** is an integrative course starting from the child's immediate world (self, family, school); **social studies** combines history, geography and citizenship.
- **Skills:** map reading, perceiving time and chronology, empathy, decision-making; **values education** is woven into activities.
- **Methods:** field trips and observation, **creative drama**, case studies, projects, concept maps; the principles of near to far and known to unknown.

## Commonly confused

- *Phonological awareness* is hearing and telling sounds apart; *letter knowledge* is recognising a sound's written symbol.
- A *misconception* is a consistent but wrong idea; a *careless error* is random.
- *Life sciences* treats the child's immediate world holistically; *social studies* combines discipline-based knowledge.

## Study tip

In each scene, first find which subject and which stage you are in, then ask whether the teacher's action follows concrete-to-abstract and pupil-centred principles. Rely only on official texts for programme details.
