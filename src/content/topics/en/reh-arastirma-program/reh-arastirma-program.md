## Core idea

A school counsellor plans services according to need, carries them out and evaluates their effect on the basis of evidence. So they need to know basic research methods and the steps of programme development. The test has 4 questions; they usually ask about the design of an example study or the stage of a programme process.

## Research designs

- **Survey:** describes an existing situation (students' level of test anxiety).
- **Correlational:** examines relationships between variables; does not prove cause and effect.
- **Causal-comparative:** explores possible causes by comparing groups that already exist.
- **Experimental:** the researcher manipulates the independent variable; experimental and control groups, pre-test and post-test.
- **Qualitative designs:** case study, phenomenology (the meaning of lived experience), grounded theory, ethnography.
- **Action research:** cyclical research by practitioners to solve a problem in their own setting.

## Programme development

- **Comprehensive developmental guidance:** a planned, preventive programme for all students; components are the guidance curriculum, individual planning, responsive services and system support.
- **Steps:** **needs assessment**, setting goals and outcomes, planning activities, implementation and evaluation.
- **Evaluation:** **process evaluation** looks at how implementation went, **outcome evaluation** at whether goals were reached; this ensures the service's **accountability**.
- **Official framework:** check the Ministry of National Education's official sources for the current structure of school guidance programmes.

## Commonly confused

- *Correlational* research shows a relationship, *experimental* research establishes cause and effect.
- *Process evaluation* happens during implementation, *outcome evaluation* measures impact at the end.

## Study tip

In design questions ask "does the researcher intervene in a variable?": if yes, it is experimental; if not, it is one of the descriptive or correlational designs.
