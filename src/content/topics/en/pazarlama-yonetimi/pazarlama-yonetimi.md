## Core idea

Marketing is the process of identifying customer needs and offering goods, services and ideas that meet them better than competitors, through **exchange**. The starting point is the **customer**, not the product. The test has **8 questions**; they usually describe a practice and ask which philosophy, strategy or element of the marketing mix it is.

## Evolution of marketing philosophies

- **Production concept:** demand exceeds supply; producing cheaply and in bulk is enough.
- **Product concept:** a good product sells itself.
- **Selling concept:** whatever is produced must be sold through aggressive selling.
- **Marketing concept:** customer needs are identified first and products are made to fit; profit comes through customer satisfaction.
- **Societal marketing:** besides satisfaction and profit, society's long-term well-being is considered.

## Consumers and target markets

- **Buying decision process:** need recognition, information search, evaluation of alternatives, purchase, post-purchase behaviour.
- **Segmentation bases:** **geographic**, **demographic** (age, income, gender), **psychographic** (lifestyle, personality), **behavioural** (usage rate, loyalty, benefits sought).
- **Targeting:** **undifferentiated** marketing offers one mix to the whole market; **differentiated** marketing offers a separate mix to each segment; **concentrated** marketing focuses on a single segment.
- **Positioning:** giving the product a distinct place in the consumer's mind relative to competitors.

## Product and price

- **Consumer products:** **convenience** (bought often with little effort), **shopping** (bought after comparison; *beğenmeli*), **specialty** (special effort for a brand; *özellikli*), **unsought** (such as insurance).
- **Product life cycle:** **introduction** (low sales, no or negative profit), **growth** (sales and profit rise fast, competitors enter), **maturity** (sales peak, competition is fiercest), **decline**.
- **BCG matrix:** by market growth rate and relative market share: **star** (high-high), **cash cow** (low growth, high share), **question mark** (high growth, low share), **dog** (low-low).
- **Ansoff matrix:** existing product-existing market is **market penetration**, existing product-new market **market development**, new product-existing market **product development**, new product-new market **diversification**.
- **Pricing:** **cost-plus** (adding a margin to cost), **price skimming** (*kaymak fiyatı*: launching high and lowering over time), **penetration pricing** (low price to gain share fast), **psychological pricing** (such as 99.90 TL).

## Distribution and promotion

- **Distribution channel:** the route a product takes from producer to consumer, direct or through intermediaries such as wholesalers and retailers.
- **Distribution intensity:** **intensive** (as many outlets as possible, convenience goods), **selective** (a limited number of intermediaries, shopping goods), **exclusive** (one dealer per area, luxury goods).
- **Promotion mix:** **advertising** (paid, non-personal communication), **personal selling** (face to face), **sales promotion** (short-term incentives such as coupons, samples and discounts), **public relations**, **direct marketing**.
- A **push strategy** aims effort at intermediaries; a **pull strategy** creates consumer demand that pulls the product through the channel.
- **Services marketing:** services are **intangible**, **inseparable** from production and consumption, **variable** and **perishable** (cannot be stored). The mix therefore extends to 7P: people, process, physical evidence.

## Commonly confused

- The *selling concept* sells what is made; the *marketing concept* makes what can be sold.
- *Price skimming* starts high; *penetration pricing* starts low.
- *Market penetration* in the Ansoff matrix is a growth strategy; *penetration pricing* is a pricing strategy.

## Study tip

Draw the matrices (BCG, Ansoff) and the product life cycle by hand and write a brand example in each cell. Questions usually give a scenario; placing it in the right cell of your drawing reveals the answer.
