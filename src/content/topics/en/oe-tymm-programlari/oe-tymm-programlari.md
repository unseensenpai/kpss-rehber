## Core idea

The curricula used from 2024 onwards were renewed within the **Türkiye Century Education Model** (*Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli*, TYMM), and the special education programmes are built on the same framework. For the model's core concepts see the site's [Türkiye Century Education Model](/kpss-rehber/en/topic/egitim-bilimleri/maarif-modeli/) topic. There are **2 questions**; they are likely to ask how the model's principles apply in special education. Check the Ministry of National Education's current programme texts for details; this page only summarises general principles.

## Common framework

- The programmes rest on the model's **common text** (*ortak metin*): **holistic education**, treating knowledge, skills, dispositions and values together.
- Aims are written as **learning outcomes**, which combine **conceptual skills**, **field skills** and the subject's content framework.
- **Dispositions** and **values** are part of the programme alongside knowledge.

## In special education

- For a student with special educational needs, the general programme is individualised through the **IEP** (*BEP*); learning outcomes are selected, broken into steps or adapted according to the student's present level of performance.
- **Differentiation:** the model's view of differentiation provides for support or enrichment according to the student; both gifted students and those needing support fall under it.
- **Functional and independent living skills:** transferring what is learned to daily life is a priority in special education.
- **Assessment:** process-focused assessment based on evidence of the student's own progress.

## Commonly confused

- The *general programme* is the common framework for all students; the *IEP* is that framework individualised for one student.
- *Support* is extra help with outcomes the student has not reached; *enrichment* goes beyond the outcome.

Learn the concepts on the Maarif Model page first; questions here mostly apply those concepts to a practice in a special education classroom.
