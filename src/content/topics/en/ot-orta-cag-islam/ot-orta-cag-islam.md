## Core idea

Medieval Islamic history tells how a faith born in Arabia quickly became a civilisation stretching **from al-Andalus to Central Asia**, and how political leadership passed over time to the **Turks**. The test has 7 questions; they usually ask about the order of events, which caliph or state a practice belongs to, and the institutions of the Turkic-Islamic states.

## The Prophet and the four caliphs

- Revelation in **610**, the **Hijra** to Medina in **622**; the **Constitution of Medina** set out relations between the city's communities.
- **Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627)**; with **Hudaybiyya (628)** the Meccans recognised the Muslims as a political party; **conquest of Mecca (630)**; death in **632**.
- **Abu Bakr:** put down the *ridda* (apostasy) wars; the Qur'an was gathered into one book.
- **Umar:** conquest of Iran, Syria and Egypt; the **diwan** system and the **Hijri calendar**.
- **Uthman:** the Qur'an was copied and sent to the main centres; first naval activity.
- **Ali:** the battles of the **Camel** and **Siffin**; after the arbitration the **Kharijites** appeared.

## Umayyads and Abbasids

- **Umayyads (661–750):** capital Damascus; the caliphate became hereditary rule; **Arab supremacy** and discrimination against non-Arab Muslims (**mawali**); **Karbala (680)**; crossing into al-Andalus in **711**.
- **Abbasids (750–1258):** capital **Baghdad**; equal treatment of the mawali, translation work at the **House of Wisdom** (*Bayt al-Hikma*); Turks rose in the army and administration (Samarra). The Mongols took Baghdad in **1258**.
- The **Andalusian Umayyads** made Córdoba a centre of learning; the **Fatimids** were the Shiite state that founded Cairo.

## Turkic-Islamic states

- **Karakhanids:** the first Turkic state to accept Islam, under **Satuk Bughra Khan**; the **Kutadgu Bilig** (Yusuf Khass Hajib) and the **Diwan Lughat al-Turk** (Mahmud al-Kashgari) date from this time.
- **Ghaznavids:** **Mahmud of Ghazni**'s campaigns in India; defeated by the Seljuks at **Dandanakan (1040)**.
- **Great Seljuks:** **Tughril Beg** entered Baghdad and freed the caliph from the Buyids; **Alp Arslan**'s victory at **Manzikert (1071)** opened Anatolia; under **Malik Shah** and the vizier **Nizam al-Mulk** the **Nizamiyya madrasas** and the **iqta** system spread; the defeat at **Qatwan (1141)** sped up decline.

## Crusaders, Mongols and civilisation

- The **Crusades** began in **1096**; **Saladin** took Jerusalem after **Hattin (1187)**. The **Mamluks** stopped the Mongols at **Ain Jalut (1260)**.
- **Science:** al-Khwarizmi (algebra), al-Farabi, Ibn Sina (*The Canon*), al-Biruni; **waqf** (endowment) and **madrasa** institutions spread learning.

## Commonly confused

- The *diwan* belongs to Umar, *copying the Qur'an* to Uthman, *gathering the Qur'an* to Abu Bakr.
- The *Umayyads* stood for Arab supremacy; the *Abbasids* for equality of the mawali.
- *Dandanakan* began the Seljuk state; *Manzikert* began the settlement of Anatolia.
