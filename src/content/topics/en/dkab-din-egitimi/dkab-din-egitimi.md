## Core idea

**Religious education** is the planned process by which a person gains religious knowledge, attitudes and values; **religious instruction** is its teaching side at school and course level. A religious culture teacher must know which approach, for which age group and with which method to teach. The test has 2 questions; they usually ask about approaches, the legal basis and classroom methods.

## Approaches

- **Confessional (from within) approach:** aims for students to live the beliefs and worship of a particular religion as its members.
- **Learning about religion (from outside):** presents religions in a neutral, knowledge-centred way.
- **Learning from religion:** aims for students to reflect on religions and enrich their own search for values and meaning.
- **Turkey's DKAB course** (Religious Culture and Ethics): knowledge-centred and **non-denominational**, centred on Islam while also covering other religions.

## Religious education in Turkey

- ***Tevhid-i Tedrisat*** **(Law on Unification of Education, 1924):** placed all schools under the Ministry of Education; imam-hatip schools were opened the same year.
- **1982 Constitution, Article 24:** religious culture and ethics is among the compulsory courses in primary and secondary education.
- **Institutions:** **imam-hatip** schools, **Qur'an courses** run by the Presidency of Religious Affairs, and elective courses (The Holy Qur'an, The Life of the Prophet, Basic Religious Knowledge).

## Development and method

- **Childhood:** concrete examples, stories, play and **role modelling**; abstract concepts are kept limited.
- **Adolescence:** abstract thinking develops; open discussion, case studies and problem solving suit this stage.
- **Methods:** lecture, question and answer, drama, case study, concept maps, field trips and observation; understanding should take priority over memorisation.
- **Values education:** fostering respect, responsibility, justice and solidarity through the teacher's attitude and the classroom climate.

## Commonly confused

- *Religious education* is a broad, lifelong process; *religious instruction* is planned teaching within school and lessons.
- The *confessional* approach aims to sustain a religion, *learning about religion* aims to introduce religions.

## Study tip

In the classroom scene given in a question, identify the students' age and the aim of the activity; the right approach or method usually follows from these two facts.
