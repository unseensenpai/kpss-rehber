## Core idea

Team sports (*takım sporları*) are sports in which two teams play in a shared area or on either side of a net, combining **cooperation and competition with opponents**. They all share one logic: the team with the ball **attacks**, the team without it **defends**. The test has **5 questions**; they usually ask about well-established basic rules, basic techniques and teaching approaches.

## Teaching and common principles

- **Teaching Games for Understanding (TGfU):** a simplified game is played first, tactical awareness develops, and technique grows out of that need.
- **Principles of attack:** keep the ball, create space, move forward. **Principles of defence:** close down space, win the ball back.
- In **man-to-man defence** each player marks an opponent; in **zone defence** each guards an area.

## The sports

- **Football:** 11 players per team on the pitch; two halves of 45 minutes. **Offside**, penalty kicks and direct and indirect free kicks are basic rules.
- **Basketball:** 5 players on court; under FIBA rules, 4 quarters of 10 minutes. Exceeding the step limit with the ball is **travelling**, and dribbling again after ending a dribble is a **double dribble**. Attack time is limited by the **shot clock**.
- **Volleyball:** 6 players on court; every rally scores a point. A set ends at 25 points with at least a 2-point lead; the deciding set is played to 15. A team may touch the ball at most **3 times** (the block does not count). A team that wins the serve **rotates clockwise**. The **libero** is a defensive specialist. Basic techniques: **set (overhead pass)**, **forearm pass (dig)**, serve, spike, block.
- **Handball:** 7 players on court (including the goalkeeper); two halves of 30 minutes. A player may take at most **3 steps** and hold the ball for at most **3 seconds**. Only the goalkeeper may enter the **goal area**; certain fouls are punished with a **7-metre throw**.

## Commonly confused

- *Man-to-man defence* follows opponents; *zone defence* follows areas.
- In volleyball the *set* is used for high, precise passes, the *forearm pass* for low or hard-hit balls.
- In handball the *goal area* belongs to the goalkeeper alone; in football players may also play inside the *penalty area*.

## Study tip

Make one table for the four sports with player numbers, playing time and the two most basic violations. For current rule details, check the latest rulebook of the relevant international federation.
