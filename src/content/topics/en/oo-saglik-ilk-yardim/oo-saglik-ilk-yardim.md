## Core idea

A preschool teacher is responsible for protecting children's health, noticing signs of illness and giving the **right first response** in an emergency. The test has 2 questions; they usually ask what to do in an accident scene or which measure to take against infectious diseases.

## Health and prevention

- **Growth monitoring:** height and weight are tracked regularly; deviations from expected development are reported to the family.
- **Nutrition:** balanced, varied food; regular meals and healthy snack habits.
- **Immunisation:** vaccines follow the Ministry of Health's current vaccination schedule.
- **Infectious diseases:** in illnesses such as chickenpox or measles the sick child stays away from the group until recovery; handwashing and hygiene are the most effective measures.
- **Safe environment:** small parts, sharp tools, medicines and cleaning products are kept out of reach; furniture is fixed in place.

## First-aid principles

- **Protect-Report-Rescue** (*Koru-Bildir-Kurtar*): first make the scene safe, then call **112**, then help.
- A first aider does not diagnose or give medicine; the aim is not to make things worse.
- **Choking:** if the child can cough, encourage coughing; with full obstruction use **abdominal thrusts (Heimlich)** for an older child and **back blows and chest thrusts** for an infant.
- **Nosebleed:** tilt the head slightly forward and pinch the soft part of the nose; do not tilt the head back.
- **Burn:** cool the area under cool running water; do not apply oil, tomato paste or toothpaste.
- **Poisoning:** do not induce vomiting; call 112 or the National Poison Information Centre (114).
- **Fracture:** do not move the limb; immobilise it as it is.

## Commonly confused

- *Infants* get back blows and chest thrusts, *older children* abdominal thrusts.
- In a *nosebleed* the head goes forward; tilting it *back* is common but wrong.

## Study tip

First-aid guidance is updated over time, so check current official first-aid sources. Pay special attention to "what should not be done" options.
